The integral of a power of a polynomial of degree near 200, times `e^x`, takes more than a minute: the page freezes for that time.

## Witness

At commit `744796485` with the CE-1307 change applied (measured with
`npx tsx`, the time of `evaluate()` alone):

```ts
const ce = new ComputeEngine();
ce.parse('\\int (x^{2}+1)^{100} e^{x}\\,dx').evaluate(); // degree 200: 72.6 s
ce.parse('\\int (x^{2}+1)^{50} e^{x}\\,dx').evaluate();  // degree 100: 11.2 s
```

Both give the correct closed form. At degree above 200 the rule declines and
the integral stays unevaluated in about 4 s.

## Cause and options

The closed form comes from `tryPolyTimesLinearExpTrig()` in
`src/compute-engine/symbolic/antiderivative.ts` (CE-1282), which accepts a
degree up to `MAX_POLY_TIMES_LINEAR_KERNEL_DEGREE = 200`. A CPU profile of the
degree-100 case gives about 3.3 s in the antiderivative search and about
7.4 s in a `simplify()` of the result that the `Integrate` evaluation in
`calculus.ts` runs; most of that time is in `expand()` and `add()` of the
101 terms.

- (a) Lower `MAX_POLY_TIMES_LINEAR_KERNEL_DEGREE` so that the rule and the
  simplification of its result stay within about a second; above the bound
  the integral stays unevaluated.
- (b) Return the closed form without the later `simplify()` when the rule
  gives it, and keep the bound.
