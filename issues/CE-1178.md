A traffic simulation can stop as too slow when its update is put in a helper row.

## Witness

On CE 0.184.0, the instrument compares direct Prelude calls with `P = roadstep(S,a,v)` and action `S → P, H → roadtrail(H,P)`, through Tycho's warmed firing environment. Direct: two compiled right sides, 0.28–0.93 ms evaluation. Computed: one compiled and one interpreted, 21–41 ms; an earlier cold firing exceeded 50 ms and the browser showed “This action took too long to run”.

The computed route logs that `roadstep` reads `N` bound to a different definition in the compilation scope. A fresh-engine captured-constant compilation control succeeds. The exact offending Tycho scope/projection remains unverified; investigate Tycho's computed preparation before changing CE's scope-safety refusal.

Workaround: call the Prelude functions directly, as the new traffic Showcase does. Closure requires compiled computed calls and parity with the direct action.

## Othello witness

The Othello Prelude exposes the same boundary with `L = legal(B,B[65])` and click action `B → play(B,L[index])`. The clicked gold marker carries the correct index, but the real firing spends 51–53 ms in evaluation before either compiled or interpreted lane is counted and fails its 50 ms budget. A literal board-cell argument applies in under 1 ms. The direct Prelude action `B → play(B,choosecell(B,index))`, which finds the ordinal legal move internally, applies with one compiled right side and no interpreted right side in about 1 ms.

Instrument: `scripts/repros/2026-11-09-ce1178-othello-computed-scope.mts`. Both forms use the actual firing environment, a warm-up and the unchanged 50 ms budget. This confirms the computed-collection resolution boundary; the exact responsible scope/phase still needs investigation. The direct scalar helper is the Showcase workaround.

## Tycho half on adoption

Tycho half on adoption: rerun both instruments, then simplify the click action in `src/graph-paper/onboarding/showcase-othello.js` to use the legal-move list directly and remove choosecell and its CE-1178 marker if the action stays compiled within budget. Traffic may retain its idiomatic direct calls; verify the computed alternative in its instrument.
