On CE 0.196.0, the colours of an imported domain-colouring graph lose
saturation: where `hsv(h, s, v)` gets s ≥ 1, the pixels show 0.56 to 0.78
instead of 1. On 0.194.3 they show 1, which is what the formula gives.

## Witness

Document `art/8fnuzqxoho` (Desmos 3-D, `complex: true`): `f(z) = Σ_{n=0}^{10}
(−1)ⁿ/(2n+1) z^{2n+1}` (the degree-21 Taylor polynomial of arctan), and the
colour reader of cell 178296513, `asOklab(w)` with
`w = Hsv(p + u·Arg(f(z)), 1/|f(z)|, |f(z)|)` and `z = x + iy`. Compiled forms,
identical on both releases (code and preamble): `javascript`
`{mode: 'complex'}`

```js
const v33 = u0_f(_SYS.ir.cadd(_SYS.ir.cplx(_.x), _SYS.ir.cmul({re:0, im:1}, _SYS.ir.cplx(_.y))));
const v40 = _SYS.ir.cabs(v33);
return _SYS.asOklab(_SYS.hsv(_.p + _.u * _SYS.ir.atan2(_SYS.ir.cim(v33), _SYS.ir.cre(v33)), 1 / v40, v40));
```

and `glsl`
`_gpu_ir_lab(_gpu_ir_lch(_gpu_ir_srgb_lab(_gpu_ir_hsv(vec3(…)))))`.

**Expected.** Desmos clamps saturation and value to [0, 1]. Inside the disc,
|f| < 1, so the saturation 1/|f| clamps to 1 and every colour is fully
saturated; the value is |f|. The Desmos reference shot of the document
(`_TASK/desmos/desmos-corpus/reference-shots-nochrome/art-8fnuzqxoho.png`)
follows this to within 0.03.

**Measured** (Tycho in headed Chromium, ANGLE Metal; median over 16 directions
at each radius):

| \|z\| | \|f\| | formula S, V | 0.194.3 S, V | 0.196.0 S, V |
| ----- | ----- | ------------ | ------------ | ------------ |
| 0.10  | 0.10  | 1.00, 0.10   | 1.00, 0.18   | 0.56, 0.28   |
| 0.30  | 0.30  | 1.00, 0.30   | 1.00, 0.54   | 0.71, 0.60   |
| 0.60  | 0.62  | 1.00, 0.62   | 1.00, 1.00   | 0.78, 1.00   |
| 0.85  | 0.86  | 1.00, 0.86   | 1.00, 1.00   | 0.74, 1.00   |

On 0.196.0 about 50 of 255 is added to the lowest channel of each pixel: a
pure green `(3, 150, 1)` becomes `(52, 162, 51)`. The value that is too high
on both releases (about 1.8·|f|) and the light-blue tint outside the disc are
Tycho's (Tycho `requirements/todo/DESMOS_ISSUES.md` D-381) and are not part of
this issue.

**Isolation.** Arms: Tycho `142a68106` with CE 0.194.3, Tycho `142a68106` with
CE 0.196.0, Tycho `acada5ec2` with CE 0.196.0, and a repeat of the last. The
three 0.196.0 renders are pixel-identical, and the 0.194.3 render was identical
to its own repeat. So the change follows the CE package, not Tycho, and it is
not noise.

Checked and unchanged between the two packages: the generated code and
preamble of all five compiled records of the document; the GLSL helpers
`_gpu_ir_hsv`, `_gpu_ir_srgb_lab`, `_gpu_ir_lch`, `_gpu_ir_lab` (source text);
the JavaScript runtime `hsv` and `asOklab` (same source up to minifier names);
the compiled `f(x + iy)`, `Arg` and `Abs` in complex mode at five points (same
values to the last digit). The cause is therefore outside the generated code:
for example a helper the renderer calls that is not in the record, or a value
that Tycho reads through another API.

**Instrument.** `node scripts/repros/2026-11-21-ce1649-domain-colouring.mjs`
in the Tycho tree renders the document with the current build (or measures a
screenshot given with `--shot`) and prints READY when the median saturation at
0.1 ≤ |z| ≤ 0.85 is at least 0.95 (exit 0), NOT READY otherwise (exit 1). It
gives READY on the 0.194.3 shot and NOT READY (0.73) on the 0.196.0 shot.

**Priority.** A wrong colour with no error (`critical`), in documents that use
`hsv` colours with full saturation (`medium`): P2 by the table, P1 with the
regression flag.

## Ask

Which change between 0.194.3 and 0.196.0 lowers the saturation of these
colours? Restore saturation 1 where `hsv` clamps it to 1.

## Decision

User decision 2026-11-21: the mathematics decides, not the look. The 0.196.0
colours inside the disc look more vivid but are less saturated than the formula
gives, so they are a wrong value. The light-blue colour outside the disc is
wrong too, but it is the same on 0.194.3 and belongs to Tycho (D-381).

## Withdrawn by Tycho (2026-11-21)

The isolation above was wrong. During ROADMAP MG phase B, Tycho rendered the
document four times on CE 0.197.0, with the instrument's saturation
measurement: 0.73 (Tycho `25fb3573c`), 1.00 and 0.60 (two runs of one Tycho
change that does not touch colours), and 0.73 (the same change with one
condition reverted). The hue is identical in the four renders; only the weight
of a light-blue veil over the disc changes, and the same light blue fills the
stage outside the disc. So the desaturation comes from Tycho's rendering and
varies between runs on one CE package; the earlier arms only happened to split
by package. Tycho fixed the rendering as D-381 (Tycho commit `214aa96e4`: the
still-frame accumulation kept frames drawn before the per-fragment colour
shader compiled). Nothing is asked of CE.
