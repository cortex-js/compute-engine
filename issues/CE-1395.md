`N(jacobiTheta(…))` gives a result to machine precision, about 16 digits, where the other special functions give the digits of the engine's precision.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 64a46834c: `N(jacobiTheta(3, 0, i))` prints `1.086434811213308`
and `N(jacobiTheta(3, 1/2, i/2))` prints `0.587974282891712`; at the same
precision `N(dedekindEta(i))` prints `0.768225422326056659003` and
`N(besselJ(0, 1))` prints 21 digits too.

## Cause and options

`JacobiTheta` has only a machine-precision evaluation. Fix: a bignum
evaluation above machine precision, or the head stays unevaluated there, as
the `DedekindEta` handler does (`special-functions.ts` around line 1269:
"the digits asked for, or the head stays unevaluated").
