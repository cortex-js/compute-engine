The tangent, cotangent and their hyperbolic forms of a complex number with a large part evaluate to `0` or `NaN`.

## Witness

At `292613616` (0.187.0), `ce.box(…).N()`:

| Program | The engine gives | The value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Coth(Complex(1000, 0.5))` | `0` | about `1` |
| `Tanh(Complex(1000, 0.5))` | `NaN` | about `1` |
| `Tan(Complex(0.5, 1000))` | `NaN` | about `i` |
| `Cot(Complex(0.5, 1000))` | `0` | about `-i` |
| `Coth(Complex(10, 0.5))` | `1.0000000022272921 − 3.4688019447535086e-9 i` | right |
| `Tan(Complex(0.5, 10))` | `3.468801929301438e-9 + 0.9999999977727079 i` | right |

`evaluate()` gives the same. Compiled JavaScript gives `NaN` for the same
inputs (the known-limitations list of `docs/COMPILATION-MODEL.md`), and for
the real `Coth(1000)`, where the interpreter gives `1`.

## Cause and options

The complex forms are computed as a quotient of `sinh`/`cosh` or `sin`/`cos`
values that overflow beyond a part of about 710. Option: for a large part,
use the form that does not overflow (`tanh(z) = (1 − e^(−2z)) / (1 + e^(−2z))`
for a positive real part, and the matching forms for the others), in the
interpreter's kernels and in the runtime helpers of compiled code
(`_SYS.ctanh` and the others).
