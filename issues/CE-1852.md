The numeric value of a limit that does not exist gives the limit from one side.

## Witness

```
Limit(Abs(x)/x, 0)      // stays unevaluated: the one-sided limits differ
N(Limit(Abs(x)/x, 0))   // 1, where it must be NaN or stay unevaluated
```

Measured at 074e6347f39717e4f6d5b44521c5c459b6d9d2b7.

## Cause and options

The numeric fallback of `Limit` (`numericLimitValue`) defaults the direction to 1 (from above), not to the two-sided limit. With no direction, compute both one-sided limits numerically and give a value only when they agree. `NLimit` documents "from above" as its default and can stay as it is.
