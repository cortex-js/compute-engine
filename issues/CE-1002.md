Sorting a mapped list whose every element is "nothing" gives a list of two elements instead of an empty list.

## Witness

`Sort(Map(x ↦ Nothing, [1, 2]))` gives `[Nothing, Nothing]` with count 2, and
`IsEmpty` of it is `False` (checked 2026-11-06). It should be `[]`. The old
entry said the result stayed unevaluated.

## Cause and options

By the user decision of 2026-10-20 (CE-74), making a lazy view into a list
erases its `Nothing` elements. `Sort` keeps them. Fix in the `Sort` handler
(`library/collections.ts`): drop `Nothing` elements, as a list literal does.
