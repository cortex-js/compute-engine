The nightly random test of the compiler fails with generator seeds other than its default one: five of seeds 1 to 6 give cases it does not classify, and one of them is an error during compilation.

## Witness

`CE_NIGHTLY=1` run of `test/compute-engine/ir-slice-random.test.ts` with the generator seed set to 1 to 6 instead of 20261112, on main `109fe5fafedeaebc85137aed808f34546fa3cbef` (the default seed passes):

- Seed 2: compilation throws "The type handler of "Mod" modified engine state" on
  `["Mod",["WithRandomSeed",42,["f",["At","M",-2],["Product","k1569",["Limits","k1569",2,3]]]],["Floor",["Negate",["g",-1.25]]]]`.
  An engine error, not a decline: the most important case.
- Seed 6: `Less(2π, n^-1.25)` at n = 0 compiles to `true`, the interpreter gives `false`. The compiled `0^-1.25` is an infinity: a pole, within the caveats; `classify()` does not flag a pole of `Power`.
- Seed 1: `Mod(Mod(e, y), π)` at y = -1e-300 gives π compiled and 0 in the interpreter.
- Seed 3: `Power(y, Mod(y, Ceil(0.5)))` at y = -1e-300.
- Seed 4: a `Map` over an empty `Filter`.

## Cause and options

Seed 2 is an engine defect (a type handler that writes engine state during a compilation); fix it. Seeds 1, 3 and 6 look like ill-conditioned points or poles, which the contract does not cover: make `classify()` recognise them (a pole of `Power` at 0 with a negative exponent; a `Mod` by a subnormal-scale divisor). Seed 4 needs a look: it may be a wrong shape. Then run the nightly test on a few seeds, not one.
