A polyline made by joining a list of points with one more point draws nothing or is computed point by point.

## Witness

`S: list<tuple<real, real>>`. Compile to javascript:
`ListJoin(PointList(PointX(S), PointY(S)), PointList(0, 0))`.

0.194.3: declines, "PointList(0, 0): a tuple where a list is read". The
interpreter appends the point (`[(1, 2), (3, 4), (0, 0)]`), and 0.186.0 compiled
the same value. Control: `ListJoin(…, List(PointList(0, 0)))` compiles. Desmos
`join(L, (0, 0))` gives this form.

Found by the Tycho codegen audit of 2026-11-19: the same MathJSON, with the same options, compiled to JavaScript on CE 0.186.0 and declines on 0.194.3 and on the CE main build of 2026-11-19 16:57, in the document context and on a bare engine. Tycho's instrument `scripts/repros/2026-11-19-ce1560-js-decline-regressions.mts` prints one READY / NOT READY line per issue (READY on 0.186.0, NOT READY on 0.194.3).

## Cause and options

`ListJoin` lowers a tuple operand as a list of its components. Option: when the
other operands are lists of tuples of the same arity, read a tuple operand as a
list of one element, as the interpreter does.
