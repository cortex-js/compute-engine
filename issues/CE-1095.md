Above machine precision, the sum or product of two lists, and the product of two matrices, keep only about 16 digits of each element.

## Witness

At precision 30 (`npx tsx scripts/probe.ts --precision 30 '<latex>'`):

```
\lbrack 0.123456789012345678901234567, 1.5\rbrack\cdot\lbrack 2, 1\rbrack
evaluate → [0.24691357802469136, 1.5]

\lbrack 0.123456789012345678901234567, 1.5\rbrack+\lbrack 2, 1\rbrack
evaluate → [2.12345678901234568, 2.5]

\begin{pmatrix}0.123456789012345678901234567 & 1\\ 2 & 3\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}2 & 0\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}
evaluate → [[0.24691357802469136, 1], [4, 3]]
```

The same operations on the scalars keep all the digits:
`2\cdot 0.123456789012345678901234567` is
`0.246913578024691357802469134`.

## Cause and options

Each route packs the lists into a tensor (`packTensor()`,
`boxed-expression/tensor-view.ts`), whose cells are doubles (or complex
values of two doubles), and computes on the cells: the element-wise product
of two vectors in `mulTensors()` (`boxed-expression/arithmetic-mul-div.ts`),
the element-wise sum in `addTensors()` (`boxed-expression/arithmetic-add.ts`),
and the matrix product in `MatrixMultiply` (`library/linear-algebra.ts`). A
big-number float element loses the digits that a double does not hold.

The first fix of CE-1048 changed only the product of a scalar and a vector
or a matrix (`scaleTensor()`), with a test of the elements,
`holdsWideBigFloat()` (commit `d4a79a39a16b309084fa032e1fbf599a5f4eae48`): when
an element is a float whose decimal is not the decimal of a double, the
route computes on the boxed elements. It was undone before 0.183.0, because
one step that mixed that route and these routes alternated between two
values (see CE-1048). Options: (a) use the same test in `scaleTensor()` and
the three routes above in one change, and compute on the boxed elements (for
`MatrixMultiply`, the sums of products of the boxed cells); (b) make the
packed tensor keep big decimals (an `expression` cell type for such an
element). Option (a) follows the decision of CE-1048; fix both issues
together.
