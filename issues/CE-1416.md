A categorical palette is interpolated like a gradient: `colormap("tycho11", 0.05)` is a mix of two category colors.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 3400041f5: `colormap("tycho11", 0.05)` gives a color between the first two
categories, and `colormap("tycho11", 3)` gives the first, the middle and the last
colors. The description of `Colormap` ("Sample colors from a named palette")
does not distinguish the two kinds of palette.

## Cause and options

`Colormap` (`library/colors.ts`, around line 719) interpolates every palette.
Option (a): for a categorical palette (`CATEGORICAL_PALETTES`), a number in
[0, 1] picks the nearest category, and an integer `n` gives the first `n`
colors. Option (b): keep the interpolation and document it. (a) is the usual
meaning of a categorical palette.
