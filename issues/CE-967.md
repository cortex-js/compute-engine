Two divergent integrals of the same kind give different results: one gives +∞ and the other `NaN`.

## Witness

```
∫ (−ln t)^{-1.001}/t dt       near 0: .N() gives +∞
∫ −t^{0.00001}/(t ln t) dt    near 0: .N() gives NaN, should be +∞
```

## Cause and options

The divergence test reports both integrands divergent, but the sign read of a
detected divergence (`numerics/gauss-kronrod.ts`,
`numerics/endpoint-quadrature.ts`) does not give the same verdict for the two.
Fix: take the sign from the sign of the shells that diverge.
