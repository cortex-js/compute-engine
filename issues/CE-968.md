Two tests pin a numeric inaccuracy that the engine no longer has, so a correct result is skipped or marked as a defect.

## Witness

```
Arccos(0.99999999999999999999) at precision 50
  engine:    1.4142135623730950488028672355116756577770092676309e-10
  bc, 100 digits: 1.41421356237309504880286723551167565777700926763088e-10
  test ref:  1.414213562373095048802867235511675657777007950265775e-10
```

## Cause and options

The engine is correct to 50 digits. The reference in
`test/compute-engine/nightly/fixtures/kernel-refs.json` (case
`acos_near1_b_p50`, from `gen_kernel_refs.py` with `mp.dps = 60`) loses about
20 digits to the cancellation of `1 − x²`. Fix: compute the reference at a
higher `mp.dps` and remove the skip in `mpmath-kernels.test.ts`.

Also (old CE-206.3): the snapshot of "Multiply: One inexact" in
`arithmetic.test.ts` has `@fixme eval-big should be same or better`. Its
21-digit result is the exact product for the decimal `1.1`; remove the
`@fixme` after a check. `needs-review`: the defect is in the tests, not the
engine.
