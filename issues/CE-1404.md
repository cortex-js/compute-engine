Two regular expressions that differ only in their flags do not compare as unequal: `regExp("a.c") == regExp("a.c", "i")` stays unevaluated.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 412113c86: `regExp("a.c") == regExp("a.c", "i")` stays as written;
it must give False. `regExp("a.c") == regExp("a.c")` gives True.

## Cause and options

`Equal` of two `RegExp` values seems to decide only identity. Fix: two
`RegExp` values are equal when the pattern and the set of flags are equal, and
unequal otherwise.
