Creating an expression with eight nested folds takes about 110 ms, 3.5 times longer than in version 0.156.0.

## Witness

```
ce.box(['Function', RSK_ROW_INSERTION, '_raw'])
```

`RSK_ROW_INSERTION` is in `test/compute-engine/fixtures/rsk-row-insertion.ts`
(8 `Fold`s nested 4 deep): 30 ms on the 0.156.0 build, 106 ms on the 0.173.0
build, about 110 ms from source at `2e6479dc8` (fastest of 10 warm runs,
measured 2026-10-31).

## Cause and options

One canonical pass of the same tree with no fold handler went from 3 to 5 ms,
so part of the growth is the cost of each pass. The fold fixpoint repeats that
cost: the inner folds are rejected at every step (the `If` in their body can be
`Missing`), and each rejected fold costs 4 canonical passes in the first step
of each enclosing fold (`FoldRefinementState`, `library/collections.ts`).
Options: keep the rejected result of an inner fold across the steps of the
enclosing fold, or make one canonical pass cheaper.
