While a person drags a point whose coordinates are literals in a formula row, Graph Paper rewrites that row's text and registers the whole document again for the drag.

## Witness

Tycho `src/graph-paper/modes/document/movable-points.ts`:
`installMovablePointShadows` (line 677) replaces each dragged literal of the
row with a shadow name (`projectedRowLatex`), declares the shadow names as
inputs, and calls `reregisterMovablePointSources`, which registers every row
again. Each frame of the drag then writes the shadow values. On release or
cancel, `dropMovablePointShadows` registers the document again from the row
model.

A Document API source row is
`{id, latex, formalTypes?, resultType?, valueType?, effects?}`
(`prepare-document.d.ts`, lines 17 to 24, CE 0.201.0): nothing binds a span
of the row's text to an input.

## Cause and options

The question for CE: is there, or could there be, a way to say "the literal
at this span of row R is the input `s_1`" (or to update one literal of one
row without a new revision of every row)? Options as Tycho sees them:

- (a) a per-row literal-to-input binding in the source row, such as
  `literalInputs: [{ span: [start, end], input: "s_1" }]`;
- (b) an existing facility (`inputs`, a macro) that already covers this:
  Tycho would like to know which;
- (c) none: Tycho keeps the rewrite. After the CE Document port, the
  registration takes 0.35 of its former time on the corpus, so the cost is
  smaller than when the candidate was written.

Workaround: the rewrite and the re-registration above, which work today.
Tycho half on adoption: in `src/graph-paper/modes/document/movable-points.ts`,
remove `projectedRowLatex`, the shadow names of `installMovablePointShadows`
and the re-registrations of `reregisterMovablePointSources`, and bind the
dragged literals through the new field.
