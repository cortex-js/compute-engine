The LaTeX of `Delimiter(…, "[,]")` loses the square brackets and writes parentheses.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 412113c86:
`Latex(Hold(Delimiter(Sequence(1, 2, 3), "[,]")))` gives `(1,2,3)`; it must
give `[1,2,3]` (or `\lbrack 1,2,3\rbrack`). With `"{;}"` it gives
`\lbrace1;2;3\rbrace`, which is right.

## Cause and options

Not investigated: the serializer of `Delimiter` reads the open and close
characters of the delimiter string, and `[` falls back to the default. Fix and
test each of `()`, `[]`, `{}`, `||` and `⟨⟩`.
