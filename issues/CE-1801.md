A sum with a clause of one value stays unevaluated, and loses that clause,
when another clause takes its values from a range that depends on it.

## Witness

At `9bd5745ae3644e92be7281b1c411ad99c653c63e`,
`npx tsx scripts/probe.ts --json '["Sum","j",["Limits","i",2,2],["Element","j",["Range",1,"i"]]]'`
gives the canonical form `["Sum","j",["Element","j",["Range",1,"i"]]]`, and
the evaluation gives the same unevaluated sum. Expected: 3, the value of
`["Sum","j",["Limits","i",2,2],["Limits","j",1,"i"]]`, which keeps both
clauses. After the change of CE-1762, the clauses in the other order
(`Element` first) give the same result.

## Cause and options

`isVacuousIndexingSet()` (`library/utils.ts`), called from
`canonicalBigop()`, drops a clause of one value (`Limits(i, 2, 2)`) when no
other clause names its index. Its scan of the other clauses reads only the
bounds of a `Limits` clause, not the collection or the condition of an
`Element` clause. Option: read every operand of the other clauses except their
index (`other.ops.slice(1).some((x) => x.has(index))`). Size S.
