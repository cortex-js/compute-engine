A sum of a subscripted term over its index treats the term as a constant, so `\sum_{k=1}^3 a_k` gives `3 a_k`.

## Witness

```
ce.parse('\\sum_{k=1}^3 a_k').evaluate()                    ->  3·a_k   (probe 2026-11-06)
ce.box(['Sum', ['Subscript', 'a', 'k'], ['Limits', 'k', 1, 3]])  ->  Sum(a_k, …), evaluates to 3·a_k
expected  ->  a_1 + a_2 + a_3
```

## Cause and options

`["Subscript", "a", "k"]` canonicalizes to the fused symbol `a_k`, and the
binding of `k` by the `Sum` is lost. A correct fix needs binder-aware
canonicalization: at fusion time the canonicalizer has no scope of the
enclosing binder. Workaround: the call form `["a_", "k"]` (the Fungrim corpus
uses it). The entry called this low priority; the probe shows that the LaTeX
form a user types reaches it.
