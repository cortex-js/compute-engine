The engine has three separate answers to "is this a collection", so some operators check twice and an Epsil program cannot define a new kind of collection.

## Witness

No program: a design item (decision P8 and §7 item 6(h) of
`docs/TYPE_SYSTEM_ROADMAP.md`). About 24 sites check both answers:

```
op.isCollection || op.type.matches('collection<any>')
```

## Cause and options

The three answers: the type lattice (`collection<T>`, `list<T>`, …), the
run-time `CollectionHandlers` (`types-definitions.ts`, JavaScript only), and
the protocol registry (`engine-protocols.ts`), which has no collection
protocol. The audit of 2026-08-21 counted 69 capability checks, 70 sites that
stay on the lattice and 10 mixed sites. Protocol extension exists since
`fc5d21302` (CE-713); parameterized protocols do not.

Recommendation of the entry: keep the lattice, make `CollectionHandlers` the
implementation of two engine protocols (`Iterable`, `Indexable`), and point
the capability checks at conformance. Steps: CE-51.1, the design document;
CE-51.2, move the 69 checks.

Full text: ROADMAP.md at commit 5784d4aec.
