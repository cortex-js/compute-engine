The vector `\vec{AB}` is read as a vector over the product of `A` and `B`, and a symbol with indices on its left is read as a product.

## Witness

`\vec{AB}` parses to `OverVector(Multiply(A, B))`, and `_p^qx` to
`Multiply("_", x, Power(p, q))` (checked 2026-11-06). The tests marked
`@fixme` in `supsub.test.ts` give the expected forms.

## Cause and options

The parser has no rule for pre-subscripts and pre-superscripts, and reads the
argument of `\vec` as an ordinary product of single-letter symbols. This file
holds the former sub-item CE-305.1; CE-305.4 is CE-1004.
