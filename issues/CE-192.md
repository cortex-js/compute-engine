The hypergeometric function ₂F₁ at machine precision can be wrong from the 11th digit.

## Witness

```
Hypergeometric2F1(-6.937, -4.123, 4.354, -0.662).N()
  relative error 3.7e-11 against mpmath
```

The largest error over 2100 points measured against mpmath.

## Cause and options

The Gauss-series branch accepts up to 1e6 of cancellation
(`GAUSS_2F1_MAX_LOSS`); this point does not use the connection-formula
prefactors. A Γ ratio through log-gamma differences was tried and is less
accurate (over 3000 random 4-tuples in [−8, 16]: direct product max 22.7 ulp;
log-gamma differences max 97.8 ulp), and the real `gamma()` is up to 26 ulp
off for negative arguments. Options: a more accurate `gamma()` for negative
arguments, or a tighter loss bound for the Gauss series with another method
for the points it then refuses.
