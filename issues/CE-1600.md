A domain-coloring plot of zeta, Hurwitz zeta, Lerch Φ, polylogarithm or polygamma cannot draw on the GPU.

## Witness

On 0.194.3, with `z` complex, the GLSL target declines `Zeta(z)`
("Zeta of complex"), `HurwitzZeta(z, 1/2)` (`churwitzzeta`),
`LerchPhi(z, 2, 1)` (`clerchphi`), `PolyLog(2, z)` (`cpolylog`) and
`PolyGamma(1, z)`. Controls: the same five compile to GLSL for a real `z`,
and `Exp(z)` compiles for a complex `z`. `Gamma(z)` for a complex `z` also
declines (`cgamma`); the instrument prints it for information.

## Request

Complex GLSL kernels for the five functions, on the `vec2` representation
that `_gpu_ir_cexp` uses. Tycho's prototype writes the Euler–Maclaurin series
of ζ(z, a) with 16 terms inline, and it compiles and draws, so a fixed-term
series is enough for a plot.

Tycho half on adoption: the showcase prototypes in
docs/scratch/2026-11-19-notatio-review/prototypes/ (riemann-zeta.entry.mjs
and polygamma-poles.entry.mjs) replace the inline series with the library
function. Size M.
