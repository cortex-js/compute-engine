A domain-coloring plot of zeta, Hurwitz zeta, Lerch Φ, polylogarithm or polygamma cannot draw on the GPU.

## Witness

On 0.194.3, with `z` complex, the GLSL target declines `Zeta(z)`
("Zeta of complex"), `HurwitzZeta(z, 1/2)` (`churwitzzeta`),
`LerchPhi(z, 2, 1)` (`clerchphi`), `PolyLog(2, z)` (`cpolylog`) and
`PolyGamma(1, z)`. Controls: the same five compile to GLSL for a real `z`,
and `Exp(z)` compiles for a complex `z`. `Gamma(z)` for a complex `z` also
declines (`cgamma`); the instrument prints it for information.

## Request

Complex GLSL kernels for the five functions, on the `vec2` representation
that `_gpu_ir_cexp` uses. Tycho's prototype writes the Euler–Maclaurin series
of ζ(z, a) with 16 terms inline, and it compiles and draws, so a fixed-term
series is enough for a plot.

Tycho half on adoption: the showcase prototypes in
docs/scratch/2026-11-19-notatio-review/prototypes/ (riemann-zeta.entry.mjs
and polygamma-poles.entry.mjs) replace the inline series with the library
function. Size M.

## Also on the javascript target (2026-11-22)

On 0.198.1 the `javascript` target declines `Zeta` of a complex argument with
the same message ("Zeta of complex"): `|\zeta(\frac12+\imaginaryI t)|` with
`t` real, and `\zeta(z)` with `z` complex (the MCP `compile` tool, both
`ok: false`, code `outside-profile`).

The first Tycho Blog post (the Riemann hypothesis,
`scripts/make-blog-notebooks.mjs`) draws three pictures of zeta on the
critical strip, and each one is interpreted at every sample:

- a domain coloring, `bitmap((t, sigma) => shade(zeta(sigma + t * i)), …)` at
  280 × 40 pixels. The notebook shows the warning "its function could not be
  compiled, so it was evaluated pixel by pixel" under the picture;
- the curve of `zeta(0.5 + t * i)` in the complex plane for `t` from 0 to 34,
  about 4.5 s in a code cell;
- the graph of `abs(zeta(1/2 + t * i))`.

In a notebook formula cell, `Plot(|\zeta(\frac12+\imaginaryI x)|)` draws
nothing, and the parametric plot of the real and imaginary parts reports
"This expression could not be compiled". So the post cannot use the plot
element for zeta.

What Tycho needs here: `Zeta` of a complex argument compiles on `javascript`
(complex lane), with the interpreter's values to plot precision. The GLSL
kernel above then gives the domain coloring in the plot element.

## Status (2026-11-22)

The JavaScript part is done: `Zeta` of a complex argument compiles on
`javascript` with the interpreter's kernel (the Hurwitz kernel at `a = 1`),
in the commit that records this section. `|\zeta(\frac12+\imaginaryI t)|`
with `t` real compiles; a call takes about 0.03 ms. `PolyGamma(1, z)` of a
complex `z` still declines on `javascript`. Open: the GLSL kernels of the
first request (`Zeta`, `HurwitzZeta`, `LerchPhi`, `PolyLog`, `PolyGamma` and
`Gamma` of a complex argument).

## Status (2026-11-22, GLSL)

The GLSL kernels are done for `Zeta`, `HurwitzZeta`, `PolyGamma` (an integer
order from 0 to 24) and `Gamma` of a complex operand
(`src/compute-engine/compilation/ir/glsl-complex-special-helpers.ts`), and
`PolyGamma(n, z)` of a complex `z` compiles on `javascript` with the
interpreter's kernels (`cpolygamma`). Measured in WebGL 2 (SwiftShader)
against `N()` (`test/compute-engine/ce-1600-glsl-complex-zeta.test.ts`):
`Gamma` within 1.9e-5 relative for |Im z| <= 30; `Zeta` within 2.2e-5 of the
larger of the value and 1 for Re s from -4 to 5 and |Im s| <= 40 (absolute
6.6e-7 next to the zero 1/2 + 21.022i); `PolyGamma` of orders 0 to 3 within
3.3e-5 relative; `HurwitzZeta` within 8e-6 of the sizes of its terms, which
is up to a few percent of the value for Re s < 0 (`docs/COMPILATION-MODEL.md`,
known limitations). Open: `LerchPhi` and `PolyLog` of a complex operand still
decline on GLSL.

## Status (2026-11-22, GLSL LerchPhi and PolyLog)

`PolyLog(s, z)` and `LerchPhi(z, s, a)` of a complex operand compile on GLSL
(`_gpu_ir_cpolylog`, `_gpu_ir_clerch_phi`). Measured in WebGL 2 (SwiftShader)
against `N()`: `PolyLog(2, z)` within 1.2e-6 relative on |z| <= 6, and every
real order that was tried within 6e-6; `LerchPhi(z, 2, 1)` within 1e-6.
`LerchPhi` of another `a` is within 3e-5 inside the unit disk and NaN past
it. Each kernel gives NaN, not a wrong value, where its terms cancel past its
error budget. The regions are in `docs/COMPILATION-MODEL.md`, known
limitations. Tycho half: the showcase prototypes can replace their inline
series with the library functions (`Zeta`, `PolyLog`, `PolyGamma`).
