## Cause and options

No single cause: each release since 0.73.0 costs a few percent, spread over
boxing, binders, types and effects. The measure, the profiles and the three
options are in `docs/plans/2026-11-06-ce349-speed-against-0-73-0.md`:
(a) close, because the goal needs CE3 to become 14 times faster (recommended);
(b) a target against 0.167.0, size L; (c) a design to box fewer intermediate
expressions in `evaluate`, size XL. If nothing is decided, no batch takes it.
Read CE-351 before you change `derivative.ts` or `sortProductOperands`.
