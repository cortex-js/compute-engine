Six benchmark cases are still slower than in version 0.73.0, the worst at 0.62 times the old speed.

## Witness

Six benchmark cases run at less than 0.95 times the speed of 0.73.0. The worst
is CE4 erf-integral at 0.62 times. Measure again before work.

## Cause and options

For CE4, the cause is the growth of the integrate and simplify machinery of
the case, not the boxing cost. Measured as unprofitable, do not try again
without a new profile: the P2-2 `isSubtype` memo, the P2-4 simplify-history
scan, the `bignumRe` memo, the P3-1 `.json` cache (status table in
`PERFORMANCE_FINDINGS.md`, which is not in the repository today), both P1
differentiation levers and the `.mul()` fast-path pivot. Read CE-351 before
you change `derivative.ts` or `sortProductOperands` for speed. The cold-start
bundle size is CE-884.
