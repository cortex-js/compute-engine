`Series(LogGamma(x), x, +∞)` stays unevaluated, while the same series of `GammaLn(x)` gives Stirling's series.

## Witness

`ce.box(['Series', ['LogGamma', 'x'], 'x', 'PositiveInfinity']).evaluate()`
gives `Series(LogGamma(x), x, +oo, 5)`; with `GammaLn` it gives
`x (ln x - 1) - 1/2 ln x + 1/2 ln(2π) + 1/(12x) - 1/(360 x³) + O(x⁻⁵)`. Main
(a28dd290f) and 0.186.0. Workaround: `GammaLn`.

## Cause and options

The two names denote ln Γ, and only `GammaLn` has the asymptotic expansion.
Check the documented relation of the two operators (`LogGamma` may be the
principal branch of log Γ on the complex plane, which equals `GammaLn` for a
real `x > 0`, where the series at +∞ is taken). Fix: give `LogGamma` the same
expansion at +∞, or make one name canonicalize to the other if they are the
same function.
