The binomial coefficient of a fraction just above 1 is wrong from the 15th
digit.

## Witness

At `08c7ffa6c`, on a default engine:

```ts
const x = ce.number([2251800939585155n, 2251799813685248n]); // the double 1.0000005
ce.box(['Choose', x, 2]).N();   // 2.50000125034945034943e-7
ce.box(['Binomial', x, 2]).N(); // the same
// x·(x − 1)/2 computed exactly is 2.50000125034944526689…e-7
```

The decimal `1.0000005` gives the right value (`2.50000125000000000001e-7`).
The interval kernel `_IA.binomial` encloses the exact value; the containment
test of the interval mode (`test/compute-engine/ir-interval-containment.test.ts`)
meets the case at a point box of `Choose(x, 2)` near 1, where the reference
is this value, and pins it in `KERNEL_DEFECTS` under its own comment.

## Cause and options

Not investigated. The relative error, 2·10⁻¹⁵, is the size of a cancellation
in `x − 1` at about 21 digits: the evaluation of `Binomial` and `Choose` of a
non-integer `n` (`library/combinatorics.ts`, the shared evaluate) seems to go
through `Γ` or a decimal of the working precision where `n − 1` cancels.
For an integer `k`, the falling factorial `n(n − 1)…(n − k + 1)/k!` in exact
rational arithmetic gives the exact value. Size S.
