The dot product of a reversed list with another list is not computed: it stays as an unevaluated matrix product.

## Witness

On main `8e7495275467d61e77cc697101e93602ce579a18`:

```ts
ce.box(['Dot', ['Reverse', ['List', 1, 3, 4]], ['List', 1, 3, 4]]).evaluate().toString();
// "MatrixMultiply(Reverse([1,3,4]), [1,3,4])"  (right: 17)
```

The same with a `Rest` or `Take` operand. With a `Sort` operand it evaluates (26). The compiled code gives 17.

Seen again 2026-11-25 (CE-1736), with a lazy `Map` table as the operand:
with `T = Map(i ↦ Map(j ↦ i + j, Range(1, 3)), Range(1, 2))`,
`MatrixMultiply(T, Transpose(T))` and `Trace(T)` stay unevaluated, while
`Transpose(T)` and `Flatten(T)` evaluate. Wrapped in `ListFrom`, the product
evaluates.

## Cause and options

Not investigated. `Dot` seems to rewrite to `MatrixMultiply` without evaluating a lazy collection operand (`Reverse`, `Rest`, `Take` give lazy views; `Sort` gives a list). Fix: evaluate the operands to lists before the product.
