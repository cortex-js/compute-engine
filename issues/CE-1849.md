The dimension of the kernel of a matrix gives the number of entries of its basis vectors, not the dimension of the kernel.

## Witness

```
Kernel([[1, 2], [2, 4]])              // [[-2, 1]]: a kernel of dimension 1
Dimension(Kernel([[1, 2], [2, 4]]))   // 2, where it must be 1
```

Measured at 074e6347f39717e4f6d5b44521c5c459b6d9d2b7.

## Cause and options

The arguments of `Dimension` are evaluated first, so the branch of its handler that reads a `Kernel` (`library/linear-algebra.ts`, near the `Hom` branch) never sees one: it sees the list of basis vectors and counts its elements. Options: (a) hold the operand of `Dimension` and read a `Kernel` before evaluating it; (b) drop the `Kernel` branch and document that `Dimension` counts elements, with `Length(Kernel(m))` for the nullity.
