A `Match` or a `Rule`, saved with `toMathJson()` and read back with `ce.box()`, has a different pattern, so it matches other values.

## Witness

```
const m = ce.box(['Function',
  ['Match', 'u',
    ['MatchCase', ['Tuple', '_a', '_b'], ['Add', 'a', 'b']],
    ['MatchCase', '_', 0]],
  'u']);
m.toMathJson()   // ... ["MatchCase", ["Pair", "_a", "_b"], ...] ...
// Applied to (1, 2): 3 before the round trip, 0 after it, with no error.

ce.box(['Match', ['Add', 'y', -1], ['MatchCase', ['Add', '_x', -1], 'x']])
  .toMathJson()  // ["Match", ["Subtract", "y", 1], ["MatchCase", ["Subtract", "_x", 1], "x"]]
// Evaluates to y; read back, it evaluates to Error("match-no-case", y - 1).
```

The left side of a `Rule` has the same rewrite
(`["Rule", ["f", ["Tuple", "_a", "_b"]], …]` is written with `Pair`).
Checked 2026-11-07 at `8c4ca0e8f1c8a7a5f6a837e64e9a0da18fce6dc4` with the CE-1024 change applied (that change
keeps `Tuple` only for the patterns of `Function`, `Assign` and `Declare`).

## Cause and options

The operand of `MatchCase` that holds the pattern (and the left side of
`Rule`) is kept raw: the pattern is matched as written
(`RAW_ROLE`, `library/control-structures.ts`). The pretty MathJSON serializer
(`serializeJsonExpression()`, `boxed-expression/serialize.ts`) applies all of
its rewrites to that raw operand: `Tuple` of two elements becomes `Pair`, and
`Add(_x, -1)` becomes `Subtract(_x, 1)`. Read back, the raw `Pair` or
`Subtract` pattern does not match the `Tuple` or `Add` value.

- (a) Serialize a raw operand of a canonical node (`isRawExpression()`) with
  `prettify: false`, so that it is written as authored. This also covers the
  held operand of `Hold` and every other operator with a raw operand role;
  measure the changed snapshots.
- (b) Add the pattern operands of `MatchCase` and `Rule` to the list of
  operands that the pretty serializer writes as authored (the CE-1024 list in
  `serializeJsonFunction()`).

Workaround: `expr.json`, or `toMathJson({ prettify: false })`.
