A maximum over a set written with a subscript, such as the largest square of
1, 2 and 3, gives no value.

## Witness

At `207f1fec2368260193812106fa8dbdbc29c0316c`, `npx tsx scripts/probe.ts '\max_{x \in \{1,2,3\}} x^2'`:

```
raw       ["Max",["InvisibleOperator","_",["Element","x",["Set",1,2,3]],["Power","x",2]]]
evaluate  max(_, Element(x, Set(1, 2, 3)), x^2)
free vars _, x
```

Expected: 9 (or an unevaluated maximum with `x` bound). The `_` of the
subscript is read as a symbol, and `x` is a free variable.

## Cause and options

The LaTeX parser has no reading for a subscript on `\max` or `\min`. Read
`\max_{x \in S} f` and `\min_{x \in S} f` as `Max` and `Min` over the indexing
set, with the binding rules of `Sum` (`binding-sites.ts`), or report a parse
error. Size S for the error; M for the reading.
