The Apollonian gasket showcase no longer opens: two statements of its prelude run on the interpreter, which then fails (CE-1482).

## Witness

On CE main 933bda7b707c8d361f14e277e9d67b8a379c6c13 (contains CE-1478) the
prelude declines at two forms:

1. A list local initialized from a top-level constant:
   `Declare("allCirclesLocal", {value: allCircles})` — "a write to a constant
   (the interpreter refuses it) or to a list local other than ReplaceAt or
   AddTo of an element".
2. A local of a list-of-tuples type: `let out: list<tuple<number, number,
   number, integer>> = []` — "a local of type list<tuple<number, number,
   number, integer>>". It is the `generations` function of CE-1482's witness.

0.193.0 compiled all 32 statements (202 ms for the whole prelude).

## Expected

Both forms compile on javascript: a copy of a constant list into a local
that the loop then rebuilds, and a local list of tuples (rebuilt with
`[...take(out, i), c, ...drop(out, i)]`).

## Instrument

`npx tsx scripts/repros/2026-11-18-ce1480-ce1481-ce1482-showcase-prelude-probe.mts` in Tycho. `CE-1481: READY` when the prelude is valid and every
statement takes the compiled route. READY on 0.193.0, NOT READY on 933bda7.

## CE answer

CE (2026-11-18): P2 stands. Disposition: needs-design (the board
decides the `ask` above first). Neither witness is the form the issue names. (a) A copy of a
constant list into a local already compiles (`let allCirclesLocal =
allCircles` is copied, the constant is never written; tests added with
CE-1480). The decline comes from `allCirclesLocal = fillGap(…,
allCirclesLocal)`, which rebuilds the whole list, of tuples, whose length
changes in the loop. (b) `out = [...take(out, i), c, ...drop(out, i)]` is
also a rebuild of the whole list. Neither is an in-place element write, so
CE-1462's list locals do not reach them: compiling them needs a loop that
carries a list of a run-time length that it changes, a capability of its own
(0.193.0 had it). CE-1482 (the interpreter's `Filter` error in
`generations`) blocks the interpreter route meanwhile. No workaround in
compiled code.

## Decision

User decision 2026-11-18: (a)
