A definite integral of a rational function gives a long sum of hypergeometric terms that `simplify()` does not reduce, although the terms cancel.

## Witness

```
∫_{1/b}^{b} (1+x²)/(x²+x⁹) dx    (Rubi's antiderivative, then simplify())
```

The integration-families audit lists 8 of 578 runs (2026-10-19) whose exact
value `simplify()` cannot reduce to the reference; all 8 come from Rubi
results. Probability `medium`: the form appears only for rare integrands.

## Cause and options

`simplify()` has no rule that relates `₂F₁(1, 1/7; 8/7; −z)` to
`₂F₁(1, 1/7; 8/7; −1/z)`. Add the inversion identity of `₂F₁` for this
parameter family, checked numerically, and rerun
`npx tsx benchmarks/audit/integration-families.ts`.

The question of the old sub-item CE-141.3 (does `simplify()` evaluate a
definite integral) is CE-960.
