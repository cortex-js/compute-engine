A product of 1000 rational factors in `x` that telescopes takes about four minutes to evaluate, and its definite integral times out.

## Witness

```
Product((x + n + 1)/(x + n), Limits(n, 1, 1000)).evaluate()
  239 s, gives (x^1000 + 501500x^999 + …)/(…) at a87e90afa
Integrate(<that product>, Limits(x, 0, 1)).evaluate()
  more than 120 s; the reference is 1 + 1000·ln(2)
```

The integration-families audit at `a5e8baa22` (2026-10-19) graded this case
`exact` (whole suite 1.3 s); at `a87e90afa` the case times out (30 s) in both
configurations. Probability `medium`, not the `corpus` default: the program is
a benchmark fixture, and users rarely write such products.

## Cause and options

`Product` multiplies the factors into one quotient of degree 1000 and does not
see that consecutive factors cancel. The product is `(x+1001)/(x+1)`. Bisect
from `a5e8baa22` on this program to find the commit that removed the closed
form, and restore it before the factors are multiplied out.
