A record has no defined way to leave out a field, to mark a field optional, or to remove a key.

## Witness

No program: the entry is a design question (Q5 and Q10 of
`docs/plans/2026-10-20-bare-tuple-record-any.md`).

## Cause and options

Decide how a record field is left out, how an optional field is represented,
and whether an explicit key-removal operation exists. Also classify the custom
folds that remain: `Dot`, ordering and the structural conversions.

The design is `docs/plans/2026-11-07-ce71-record-fields.md` (2026-11-07). The
decisions of 2026-11-02 settle the classification (`Nothing` is an ordinary
element; one defect, CE-1101). It asks three decisions: A, whether a field
that holds `Missing` keeps its key; B, the spelling of an optional field;
C, whether `DeleteAt(r, "b")` removes a key.

## Decision

User decision 2026-11-07: record types are closed and dictionaries are open.
A record has exactly the fields its type lists, some of them optional; there
is no width subtyping. This replaces Q6 of
`docs/plans/2026-10-20-bare-tuple-record-any.md`. A record stays an immutable
value, so a key is removed by an expression that returns a copy, not by a
`delete r.b` statement (design §6).

User decision 2026-11-07, design approved: A (a), a field that holds
`Missing` keeps its key; B (a), an optional field is written `b: string?`;
C (b), `DeleteAt(r, "b")` returns a copy without the key. The change is §7 of
the design.
