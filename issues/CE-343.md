A Tycho plot that selects points by a condition takes about 2 ms per point, so at 10 000 points it passes the 5 s limit.

## Witness

The body of Tycho document `s8ishknvhe`, from source with `tsx` (measured
2026-09-18): 500 points 0.9–1.2 s, 2 000 points 4.2 s; at 10 000 points the
5 s limit refuses the evaluation. Tycho now binds each repeated function node
once (`shared-subexpressions.ts` in `dev/tycho`): 10 000 points 11.0 s →
1.8 s.

## Cause and options

Two causes, each an issue of its own: repeated sub-expressions are evaluated
once per reference (CE-882), and each element of a broadcast operator builds a
new function (CE-883). Remaining step (CE-343.3): measure again, on a built
bundle, the 8 µs per element per condition that the comment on
`evaluateElementwiseSelection` (`library/control-structures.ts`) quotes. The
body does not compile: `Abs` declines over a possibly list-valued operand
(CE-268).
