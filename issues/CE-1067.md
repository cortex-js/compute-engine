A product of a few hundred rational factors in `x` that do not cancel one by one takes seconds to evaluate, and gives a quotient that is not reduced.

## Witness

```
Product((x + n + 2)/(x + n), Limits(n, 1, N)).evaluate()
  N = 100: 1.7 s, N = 200: 6.7 s, gives (x^200 + 20500x^199 + …)/(…)
  the value is (x + N + 1)(x + N + 2)/((x + 1)(x + 2))
```

Measured on the CE-1028 fix. Impact `minor`: no host budget is known for
such a product.

## Cause and options

The fold of `Product.evaluate` multiplies each term into the accumulated
quotient with `mul()` (`productAccumulate()` in
`src/compute-engine/library/arithmetic.ts`), which expands the products and
does not cancel common factors. The CE-1028 fix gives the closed form only
when one factor cancels the next (`finiteTelescopingProduct()`). Options:
keep the numerator and the denominator as lists of factors in the fold and
cancel equal factors; or recognize a shift by `m > 1` in the closed form.
