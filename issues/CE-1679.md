In VS Code and on the documentation site, a LaTeX formula that spans two lines loses its color after the first line, and the rest of the formula is colored as code.

## Witness

```epsil
m = $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\
3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$
```

The lexer reads one island from the first `$` to the second
(`scanLatexIsland` in `src/epsil/lexer.ts` does not stop at a line break). The
VS Code grammar ends the island at the end of the line (`latex-island` in
`vscode-epsil/syntaxes/epsil.tmLanguage.json`: `"end": "\\$|(?=$)"`), and so
does the highlight.js mode (`LATEX_ISLAND` in `src/epsil/highlight-js-mode.js`:
`illegal: /\n/`). The second line is then read as code, and its closing `$`
opens a new island. The new `epsil-highlight` entry (CE-1670) follows the
lexer.

`plausible`: a matrix or an aligned formula is often written over several
lines. No ```epsil block of `src/epsil/docs` has such an island, so the
probability is `low`, as for a `crafted` witness.

## Cause and options

Remove the end-of-line stop from both grammars: `"end": "\\$"` in the
TextMate grammar, and drop `illegal: /\n/` from `LATEX_ISLAND`. Size S. A test
of the TextMate grammar on this program needs `vscode-textmate` and
`vscode-oniguruma`, which are not dependencies.
