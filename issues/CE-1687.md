The completion menu at the collection of a `for` loop offers the loop
variable, which is not defined there.

## Witness

On the worktree of CE-1664 (`4d645c5607b2f1bf450a4d43548e01f14df0cf56`
plus CE-1664.4):

```ts
const source = 'let xs = [1, 2]\nfor i in  {}';
epsilCompletions(ce, source, source.length - 3).items
  .filter((i) => i.kind === 'variable').map((i) => i.label);
// ➔ ["xs", "i"]
```

`i` is bound by the loop for its body only. `for i in i { print(i) }` gives no
diagnostic and prints nothing (`i` is a free symbol there).

## Cause and options

The visibility of the names (`documentBindings` / `isNameVisibleAt`, used by
`epsilCompletions` in `src/epsil/completion.ts`) probably treats the loop
variable as visible in the whole `Loop` node, also in the collection of its
`Element` clause (not read). Option: make the binding of an `Element` clause visible in the body
(and in the later clauses and the guard of a comprehension) only.
