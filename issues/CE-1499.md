A formula that holds an exact fraction, and that the host boxes without the canonical form, no longer compiles.

## Witness

`x: real`;

```
ce.expr(["Add", "x", ["Rational", 1, 256]], { form: "structural" })
```

- 0.193.0: compiles on javascript (1.00390625 at x = 1) and on glsl
  (`x + 0.00390625`).
- 4779733 (0.194.0): declines on javascript, glsl and interval-js:
  "Rational(1, 256): the operator Rational is not supported by the compiler".
  (0.193.0 declined it on interval-js too.)

Control on 4779733: the same expression in the canonical form compiles on
every target.

## Expected

`Rational(p, q)` of two integer literals is a constant in every form, as
`Rational(1, 256)` of the canonical form is. Workaround: box the expression
in the canonical form before the compilation.

`npx tsx scripts/repros/2026-11-18-ce1497-ce1498-ce1499-ce1500-ce1501-compile-gaps-probe.mts` in Tycho. `CE-1499: READY` when the structural form compiles on javascript
(with the value 1.00390625) and on glsl. READY on 0.193.0, NOT READY on
4779733.
