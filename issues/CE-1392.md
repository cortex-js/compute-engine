`sigma0(-6)` and `sigma1(-6)` stay unevaluated, while `divisorSigma(0, -6)` is 4.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 1e146ff55: `sigma0(-6)` prints `Sigma0(-6)`; `sigma0(6)` is 4
and `divisorSigma(0, -6)` is 4 (it reads `|n|`). The signatures of `Sigma0`
and `Sigma1` are `(integer) -> integer` and their descriptions say "positive
divisors of n", which a negative `n` has.

## Cause and options

Option (a): `Sigma0` and `Sigma1` read `|n|`, as `DivisorSigma` does.
Option (b): all three answer an error for `n ≤ 0`. (a) keeps the three
consistent with the least change; `n = 0` needs the same answer in all three.
