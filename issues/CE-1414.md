ask: Is a sum of quantities of two different dimensions (5 m + 3 s) an incompatible-type error (a, recommended), also with a number beside a quantity (b), or does it stay unevaluated as documented (c)?
---
Adding quantities of incompatible dimensions gives no error: `quantity(5, m) + quantity(3, s)` is `5 m + 3 s`.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 3400041f5: `quantity(5, m) + quantity(3, s)` gives `5 m + 3 s`; it must
be an error that names the two dimensions (length and time).

## Cause and options

`Add` of two `Quantity` values keeps the sum when the units do not convert.
Fix: answer an `incompatible-unit` (or `incompatible-type`) error when the
dimensions differ, and keep the sum only when a unit is symbolic.

## The contract that the fix changes

Reproduced at main `d3a9723b74` (`npx tsx scripts/probe.ts --json
'["Add",["Quantity",5,"m"],["Quantity",3,"s"]]'`): canonical, `evaluate`, `N`
and `simplify` all give `5 m + 3 s`, of type `quantity`.

This is the documented behavior today, so the fix needs a decision:

- `~/dev/cortexjs.io/docs/compute-engine/19-guide-units.md`, "Addition and
  Subtraction": "Adding quantities with incompatible dimensions (e.g., meters
  + seconds) returns the expression unevaluated."
- `quantityAddFolds()` in `src/compute-engine/library/quantity-kind.ts`
  folds each group of one dimension apart, by design (CE-508.3):
  `2 m + 3 m + 4 s + 5 s` is `5 m + 9 s`. The test "two dimensions give two
  folds" in `test/compute-engine/quantity-kind.test.ts` pins it, and the test
  "a quantity and a number are not added" pins `2 m + 1 + 2` as `2 m + 3`.

## Options

- **(a) Recommended.** A sum that holds two quantities whose units have known
  dimensions that differ is an error: `5 m + 3 s` and `2 m + 3 m + 4 s` are
  `Error("incompatible-type", "length vs time")`, the code that
  `UnitConvert(5 m, s)` gives today. A sum stays as it is when an operand is
  not a quantity or a unit has no known dimension: `x + 2 m` stays
  `x + 2 m` (`x` can hold a length), `2 m + 3` stays `2 m + 3`. The guide
  paragraph and the CE-508.3 test change. About 15 lines in
  `quantityAddFolds()` (a fold of every operand to the error when two
  dimension groups exist).
- **(b)** As (a), and a number beside a quantity whose unit is not
  dimensionless is the same error: `2 m + 3` is
  `Error("incompatible-type", "length vs dimensionless")`. Stricter; it also
  changes the test "a quantity and a number are not added".
- **(c)** Keep the documented behavior: the sum stays unevaluated. No code
  change; CE-1414 is closed as "by design", and the Epsil units reference
  (`src/epsil/docs/reference/units.intro.md`) says that the sum stays
  unevaluated.

Comparisons are out of scope for each option: `Less(5 m, 3 s)` stays
unevaluated (test "Incompatible units stay unevaluated" in
`test/compute-engine/units.test.ts`).
