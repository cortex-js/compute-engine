Adding quantities of incompatible dimensions gives no error: `quantity(5, m) + quantity(3, s)` is `5 m + 3 s`.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 3400041f5: `quantity(5, m) + quantity(3, s)` gives `5 m + 3 s`; it must
be an error that names the two dimensions (length and time).

## Cause and options

`Add` of two `Quantity` values keeps the sum when the units do not convert.
Fix: answer an `incompatible-unit` (or `incompatible-type`) error when the
dimensions differ, and keep the sum only when a unit is symbolic.
