The value of Li₁(1/2) shows as −ln(1/2) instead of ln 2.

## Witness

On 0.194.3 `PolyLog(1, 1/2).evaluate()` gives `-ln(1/2)` and
`PolyLog(1, 1/3)` gives `-ln(2/3)`; the expected forms are `ln(2)` and
`ln(3/2)`. Controls: `N()` gives 0.693147…, `PolyLog(1, -1)` gives `-ln(2)`,
and `.simplify()` of the first result gives `ln(2)`.

## Cause and options

The rule `Li₁(z) = -ln(1 - z)` builds the negated logarithm and stops. Option:
for a rational `1 - z` in (0, 1), return `ln(1/(1 - z))`, or apply the
`-ln(p/q) = ln(q/p)` rewrite that `simplify()` already has to the result of
the rule. Size S.
