The limit at infinity of a logarithm in base 10, or in another base, is computed as if the logarithm were natural.

## Witness

At commit f21620507e44be1ec76042838abae48b34abf815:

```
\lim_{x\to\infty} \log((2x+1)/x)      gives ln(2),  should be log₁₀(2) = 0.30103…
\lim_{x\to\infty} \log_{x/(x+1)}(x)   gives +∞,     should be −∞ (the base tends to 1 from below)
```

MathJSON: `["Log", ["Divide", ["Add", ["Multiply", 2, "x"], 1], "x"]]` and
`["Log", "x", ["Divide", "x", ["Add", "x", 1]]]`, each in
`["Limit", ["Function", <body>, "x"], "PositiveInfinity"]`.

## Cause and options

`limitAtPosInf()` in `src/compute-engine/symbolic/limit.ts` handles `Ln` and
`Log` in one branch, with `lnOfLimit()` of the first operand. The base (10, or
the second operand) is not read. `growthLevel()` also reads only the first
operand.

Option (recommended): rewrite `Log(u, b)` as `Ln(u)/Ln(b)` (`b = 10` for one
operand) before the strategies at infinity; or decline a `Log` whose base
depends on `x`.
