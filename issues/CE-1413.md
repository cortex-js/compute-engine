`unitConvert(quantity(12, "in"), ft)` gives `0.9999999999999998 ft`, not exactly 1 ft.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 3400041f5: `unitConvert(quantity(12, "in"), ft)` gives
`0.9999999999999998 ft`. Both factors are exact (1 in = 0.0254 m, 1 ft = 0.3048 m).

## Cause and options

The conversion multiplies and divides machine floats. Fix: keep the unit
factors as exact rationals (or decimals) and convert an exact magnitude
exactly; an inexact magnitude stays inexact.
