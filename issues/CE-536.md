Opening a large Tycho document spends 1.6 s on its 15 surface formulas, because identical parts are computed again at each copy.

## Witness

```
npx tsx benchmarks/tycho-document-open/evaluate.ts   # RUNS=, ONLY=0,2; CE_CACHE_STATS=1 prints isSubtype counts
```

Document `art/2ki2hjsouf`, 15 bodies of 2 to 67 KB: 1 623 ms with a
production-like bundle (median of 5), 1 684 ms in Tycho.

## Cause and options

- Copies: input 0 has 8 100 nodes, 244 distinct; one `ListJoin` occurs 18
  times (46 % of the time).
- `boundCallType` (`boxed-function.ts`) is 37 %: 27 000 calls on 184 keys;
  425 000 `isSubtype` calls on 1 096 pairs.
- Each `Comprehension` index write advances the `any` axis.

Fixes: (a) per `evaluate()`, a memo keyed on the structural hash of an
effect-free subtree; (b) a `boundCallType` memo keyed on the definition, the
operand types and `_cacheGeneration()`; (c) an `isSubtype` fast path for primitives.

Tycho half on adoption of (a): remove `stageSurfacePointArguments()`. Full text: ROADMAP.md at commit 5784d4aec.
