An empty `\placeholder{}` or `\smash{}` after an operand and before a minus sign gives a sequence of two expressions: `a\placeholder{}-b` is `Sequence(a, Negate(b))`, not `a - b`.

## Witness

At `7318c0c9860f65e59a60f33390cdf9e5348ae153`, with
`ce.parse(latex, { form: 'raw' }).json` and `ce.parse(latex).toString()`:

```
a\placeholder{}-b       Sequence(a, Negate(b))                              a -b
a\smash{}-b             Sequence(a, Negate(b))                              a -b
a\placeholder{}\,-b     Subtract(InvisibleOperator(a, HorizontalSpacing(3)), b)   a - b   (control)
a\phantom{x}-b          Subtract(a, b)                                      a - b   (control: a decoration)
```

The witness is crafted: an empty slot between an operand and a sign. The
workaround is to remove the empty command.

## Cause

`\placeholder{}` and `\smash{}` with an empty argument return `Nothing`, but
they are not decorations, so `skipDecorationBeforeInfix()`
(`latex-syntax/parse.ts`) does not skip them before the infix `-`. The
juxtaposition reads them as its right operand, `parsePrimary()` drops the
empty result and finds no primary at `-`, and the juxtaposition ends with no
operand. Option: in `skipDecorationBeforeInfix()`, also skip a content
command (`atContentCommand()`) whose group is empty.
