A call of `complex` that names its arguments is an error, though the signature names them.

## Witness

In Epsil, `complex(real: 1, imaginary: 2)`, `complex(1, imaginary: 2)` and
`complex(imaginary: 2, real: 1)` each give `argument-order-invalid` ("a
positional argument cannot follow a named argument"). They must give the
complex number `1 + 2i`, as `complex(1, 2)` does. The signature of `Complex`
is `(real: number, imaginary: number) -> complex`.

In MathJSON, `ce.box(["Complex", ["NamedArgument", {str: "real"}, 1],
["NamedArgument", {str: "imaginary"}, 2]])` gives
`Add(Error(argument-names-unavailable, "real"), Multiply(Error(..., "imaginary"), Complex(0, 1)))`.

Workaround: pass the arguments by position.

## Cause and options

Probable cause, not confirmed: the number fold of `Complex` in `box()`
(`src/compute-engine/boxed-expression/box.ts`, the `canonicalNumber` block,
`if (name === 'Complex')` with two operands) runs before the named-argument
seam and folds the `NamedArgument` carriers as if they were the parts, into
`Add(re, Multiply(im, i))`. In Epsil the carriers then reach `Add`, where a
named carrier comes before a positional operand: `argument-order-invalid`.

Option: skip the fold when an operand is a `NamedArgument`, so that the
normalization reorders the operands first and the fold sees the parts. The
same check may be needed for the other operators of that block (`Divide`,
`Rational`, `Negate`), whose parameters have no names today. Size S.

Epsil completion does not offer the parameter names of `complex` until this
is fixed (`namedCallee` in `src/epsil/completion.ts` names this issue).
