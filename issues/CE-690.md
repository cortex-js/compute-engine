A numeric integral next to a singular bound is much less accurate than in release 0.170.0, with a correct but wide error.

## Witness

```
N(Integrate(t ↦ cos(t/2)/√(cos t − cos ½), [0, ½]))
  today:   2.2214414411509225 ± 0.0007   (true error 2.8e-8)
  0.170.0: 2.221441469078394 ± 8.3e-12   (true error 7.9e-13)
```

## Cause and options

Next to ½, `cos t − cos ½` cancels in doubles, so the integrand is noisy.
Corner shells 25 and 26 fail the accuracy test (`SHELL_RELATIVE_ERROR`), and
shell 27 passes by chance; `extrapolateCorner()`
(`numerics/endpoint-quadrature.ts`) reads this as a feature of the integrand
(`featureAfterShells`) and refuses to extrapolate. That rule exists for a step
next to a pole. The design is a test that tells rounding noise from a small
feature: estimate the noise of `f` between neighboring doubles at the nodes,
or compare the later shells with the model of the first shells. Two other
tests were measured on 900 integrals and rejected. User decision 2026-10-30:
a design item. The error floor is CE-963. Full text: ROADMAP.md at commit
5784d4aec.
