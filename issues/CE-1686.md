The completion menu marks a variable that holds a point (a tuple of numbers)
or a quantity as not fitting at an operand of `*`, `-` or `^` and at the
argument of `sin`, although the program with that variable runs; a host that
hides such names hides them.

## Witness

On the worktree of CE-1664 (`4d645c5607b2f1bf450a4d43548e01f14df0cf56`
plus CE-1664.4), with

```epsil
let q: tuple<real, real> = (1, 2)
let d: quantity = Quantity(3, "m")
```

`epsilCompletions` gives `fit: 'never'` for `q` and `d` at `2 * ▮`, `-▮` and
`2 ^ ▮`, for `d` at `2 - ▮`, and for `q` at `sin(▮`. Each of these programs
has no diagnostic in `checkEpsil` and a value in `executeEpsil`: `2 * q` is
`(2, 4)`, `-q` is `(-1, -2)`, `2 ^ q` is `(2, 4)`, `sin(q)` is
`(sin(1), sin(2))`, `2 * d` is `6 m`, `2 - d` is `-3 m + 2`, `-d` is `-3 m`.
(`2 - q` and `2 / q` are errors, so `never` is right there.)

## Cause and options

The fit judges a name against `broadcastable<T>` of the parameter type of the
operator's signature (`number` for `Multiply` and `Subtract`,
`complex | infinity` for `Negate`, `complex | signed_infinity` for the exponent
of `Power`). The handlers also accept a tuple of numbers (component-wise) and
a quantity, which the signatures do not show. Options: (a) let the fit admit a
tuple of `T` under `broadcastable<T>` and add `quantity` to the admitted type
of the arithmetic operands in `admittedTypeAt` (`src/epsil/completion.ts`);
(b) widen the signatures of the operators. (a) touches only the completion.
