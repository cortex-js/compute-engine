A finite double integral whose integrand grows like 1/r near two points gives a value with an uncertainty of about 10⁷.

## Witness

```
\int_0^1\int_0^1 \frac{1}{\sqrt{(x-0.5)^2+(y-0.5)^2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{(x-0.25)^2+(y-0.25)^2}} dy\,dx
  .N() gives 0 ± 50000000, should be about 6.68864 (3.52549 + 3.16315)
```

The potential of two point charges over the unit square. Near each point the
area element `r dr dθ` cancels the `1/r`, so the integral is finite.

## Cause and options

`nIntegrateMultiple` (`library/calculus.ts`) cannot resolve the inner
integral on the lines through a singular point. `integrateAroundSingularPoint`
(added for CE-599) cuts the box at ONE such point and refuses two. Options:

1. Integrate each term of a sum separately.
2. Cut the box at every singular point, and each part again between two
   points, so that each part has one point at a corner.
3. Give `NaN` when the error is much larger than the estimate.
