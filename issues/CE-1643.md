The compilation guide says that `Boole` and `KroneckerDelta` do not compile, but they compile.

## Witness

`docs/COMPILATION-MODEL.md`, section "Lists of two run-time lengths", the
paragraph "A compiled comparison is two-valued", ends with: "`Boole` and
`KroneckerDelta` have no lowering and decline."

At 7fdae788e2a52354bbe69a6f3544b89afaf4e927, with `x: real` and `n: integer`:

- `Boole(x < 1)` compiles to `((_.x < 1) ? 1 : 0)` on `javascript` and to
  `((x < 1.0) ? 1.0 : 0.0)` on `glsl`.
- `KroneckerDelta(n, 2)` compiles to `((_.n === 2) ? 1 : 0)` on `javascript`
  and to `((n == 2.0) ? 1.0 : 0.0)` on `glsl`.

`src/compute-engine/compilation/ir/README.md` ("The contracts", the paragraph
"`Boole(b)` is the row `boole`") describes the current behavior.

## Expected

The sentence says what compiles: `Boole` and `KroneckerDelta` of scalars
compile; a list operand of `KroneckerDelta`, three operands or more, and
`Boole` in the interval mode decline.

## Cause and options

The sentence was not updated when the rows were added. Replace it with the
current rule, or with a pointer to the README paragraph. Size S. The defect is
in text that CE-1532 does not change.

## Code

The rows of `Boole` and `KroneckerDelta` are in `src/compute-engine/compilation/ir/operators.ts`; the text to change is in `docs/COMPILATION-MODEL.md`.
