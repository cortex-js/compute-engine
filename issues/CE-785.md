A deeply nested expression, such as a chain of 350 sines and cosines, no longer compiles and runs in the slower interpreter.

## Witness

`test/compute-engine/compile-deep-nesting.test.ts` › "a chain of 350 sines and
cosines compiles" gives `success: false` in the full Jest suite and passes alone. Horner form nests two levels per degree.

## Cause and options

The compiler is recursive. After CE-320.2 the limit was about 420 levels; at
`4c55bdbee` it is about 370 outside Jest, and less in a Jest worker that ran
other files. Find the commit that added stack frames on the path of every
operand (`compileExpr`, `_compileInner`, the reference analysis of
`compile-references.ts`), and make those frames smaller again, or change the
recursion of the deep cases to a loop. Then measure the limits again. User decision 2026-11-03: the nightly fall from
347 to 346 levels is accepted and is not a new defect.
