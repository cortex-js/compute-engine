A product of a statement with no value gives no value, where a sum of it gives a type error.

## Witness

`ce.box(['Product', 'Nothing']).evaluate()` and `.N()` give `Nothing`.
`ce.box(['Sum', 'Nothing']).evaluate()` and `Product(List(1, Nothing))` give
the `incompatible-type` error (`number` expected, `nothing` found).
`docs/ERROR-MODEL.md` ("`Nothing` is the value of an empty block", item 3):
`Sum` and `Product` do not skip `Nothing`, and an operator that needs a value
and meets `Nothing` gives the `incompatible-type` error. Found by the session
that fixed CE-1042; not caused by CE-961 (`Nothing` has no unknowns).

## Cause and options

`productAccumulate()` in `library/arithmetic.ts` returns `ce.One.mul(term)`,
and `ce.One.mul(ce.Nothing)` is `Nothing` (also `ce.Zero.add(ce.Nothing)` is
`Nothing`). `reducerElementError()` does not report `Nothing`. Option: give
the `incompatible-type` error for a `Nothing` term in `productAccumulate()`,
as the `Sum` fold does (`addSumTerm`). Check whether `mul` and `add` with a
`Nothing` operand should give the error too. Size S.
