A list written with a `tabulate` of random numbers inside it shows the formula of the `tabulate`, not its numbers.

## Witness

Checked at `c2bf6688f4f4ae23ebbc6e4946f025823dbbff7f`, in Epsil:

```
[tabulate(i => random(), 3)]      gives [Tabulate(i ↦ Random(), 3)]
[map(x => random(), [1, 2, 3])]   gives [[0.342…, 0.188…, 0.354…]]
[tabulate(i => i * 2, 3)]         gives [[2, 4, 6]]
```

The first must give a list of three numbers, as the second does.

## Cause and options

An element of a list literal that is a finite view is listed
(`listedElement()`, `boxed-expression/listed-element.ts`). A view with an
effect is listed only when its count has a bound that needs no call of its
callback (`listingCountUpperBound()`), and that function has no case for
`Tabulate`, whose count is its dimensions (`tabulateCount()`,
`library/collections.ts`). `hasConstantTimeCount()` omits it too. Fix: give
`Tabulate` its bound in both.

The same gap is one exception of CE-1807: a host function receives the
`Tabulate` view, not a `List`, for `hostSum(tabulate(i => tick(i), 3))`. The
fix removes that exception from `docs/COMPILATION-MODEL.md` ("Functions of the
host"), from the JSDoc of `evaluate` in `types-definitions.ts`, from the guide
page `06-guide-augmenting.md` of cortexjs.io, and from the test "a Tabulate
with an effect stays a view" of `host-lazy-arguments-ce1807.test.ts`.
