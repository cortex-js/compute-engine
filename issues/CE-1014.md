The hypergeometric function ₂F₁ at machine precision can be wrong from the 12th digit when z < −1 or 0.5 < z < 1.

## Witness

```
Hypergeometric2F1(10.214, 8.842, 11.991, -4.788).N()   // precision 'machine'
  relative error 2.05e-12 against mpmath (same double arguments)
Hypergeometric2F1(3.619, 1.788, 6.69, 0.653).N()
  relative error 1.83e-12
```

The two largest errors over 6000 random points after the CE-192 fix
(2026-11-06). Every error above 5e-13 is at z < −1 or z > 0.5.

## Cause and options

These points use the connection formula at 1 − z in
`hypergeometric2F1Real()`. It accepts parts up to 1e3 times the result
(`CONNECTION_2F1_MAX_LOSS`), its Γ prefactors are up to about 100 ulp off,
and its two series are summed in doubles. Options: (a) sum the two series
in double-double, as `gauss2F1Value()` does, and compute the Γ ratios more
accurately; (b) for z < −1, use the Pfaff form whose connection formula
cancels less; (c) a smaller bound, which gives NaN for some points that
have a value now.
