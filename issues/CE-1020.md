A document with a list of integrals that reads a slider opens about 3 times slower unless Tycho skips their evaluation when the document opens.

## Witness

`c(y,D,e) := \int_{-D}^{D} n(x,y,e)\,dx`, `E` a list of 6 numbers, `L` a
slider with no value, and four heads `\int_a^b y\,c(y,L,E)\,dy`. Their
`evaluate()` searches for an antiderivative and gives the integrals back
unevaluated; no consumer reads that result. Registration of the document:
53 ms when Tycho skips the four evaluations, 172 ms with them (medians of 5,
0.182.0, box lock). Reading on 0.182.0: `CE-1020: NOT READY on
0.182.0 (… 3.3×, limit 1.25×)`.

Requested: such an `evaluate()` costs about what boxing costs, for example
with no antiderivative search for an integrand that holds an unevaluated
integral: registration with the evaluations at most 1.25 times without.

Tycho half on adoption: remove the `Integrate` branch of
`_bigOperatorTermReachesValuelessVariable()` (Tycho's
`src/graph-paper/graph/document-ce-manager.ts`) and change test (a) of
`tests/node/graph-paper/slow-collections.test.ts`.

## CE answer

CE (2026-11-07): priority P3, confirmed by the board on 2026-11-07
(`minor`, `medium`, with a workaround). Disposition: pool; the batches take
P3 items in priority order, size M. In the witness the search finds no
antiderivative and returns the integral, so skipping the search for an
integrand that holds an unevaluated integral gives the same result there. The
fix must check that it gives the same result in every case (not checked yet).
Workaround: keep the `Integrate` branch of
`_bigOperatorTermReachesValuelessVariable()` until the instrument prints READY
on a release. Not measured again on 0.183.0.
