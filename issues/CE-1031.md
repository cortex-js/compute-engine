A double integral with both differentials in the numerator of a fraction, `\iint \frac{dx\,dy}{xy}`, integrates over one variable only and gives a wrong value.

## Witness

Readings of 2026-11-06, at commit `23db78e092dd67fa2ec95c360ee73dbf35e1b1ea`:

```
\iint \frac{dx\,dy}{xy}               parses to Integrate((d·x)/(x·y), Limits(y))
                                      evaluate() gives d·ln|y|
\int_1^2\int_1^2 \frac{dx\,dy}{xy}    evaluate() gives 3/2·ln 2; the value is (ln 2)^2
```

## Cause and options

The raw parse of the numerator `dx\,dy` is
`InvisibleOperator(d, x, HorizontalSpacing(3), d, y)`.
`parseFinalDiffOperators()` in
`latex-syntax/dictionary/definitions-calculus.ts` takes the last
differential, `dy`, then stops at the `HorizontalSpacing` operand, so the
`d·x` stays in the integrand. Fix: skip `HorizontalSpacing` operands in the
search, and give the differentials after the first to the enclosing
integrals as `parseIndexes()` does at the top level. The order of the two
`Limits` follows CE-296.
