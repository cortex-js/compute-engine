The simplified form of `\log(x^2)` shows `2\log_{10}|x|`, with a base that the user did not write, so it is not the same as the simplified form of `2\log(|x|)`.

## Witness

```
ce.parse('\\log(x^2)').simplify()    ->  ["Multiply",2,["Log",["Abs","x"],10]]   2\log_{10}\vert x\vert
ce.parse('\\log(x^3)').simplify()    ->  ["Multiply",3,["Log","x"]]              3\log(x)
ce.parse('2\\log(|x|)').simplify()   ->  ["Multiply",2,["Log",["Abs","x"]]]
```

The benchmark pair `\log(x^2)` and `2\log(x)` of `grudnitski.test.ts` reached
it.

## Cause and options

The `Log` power rule in `src/compute-engine/symbolic/simplify-log.ts` builds
`Log(x, 10)` in the odd branch and `Log(Abs(x), 10)` in the even branch. A
later step removes the base 10 from the first form but not from the second;
that step is not traced. Options: find that step and make it accept `Abs(x)`,
or make the canonical form of `Log(x, 10)` drop the base 10. Size S.
