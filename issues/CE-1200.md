The type of a deep chain of calls is quadratic in its depth: the `isFinite` getter of a function expression is not cached.

## Witness

```
let e = ce.box('x');
for (let i = 0; i < 20000; i++) e = ce.function('Sin', [e]);
```

`test/compute-engine/ce-882-shared-copies.test.ts`, "A held expression deeper
than the call stack evaluates": 6 s alone with `CE_BINDING_UNITS=0`, 38 s alone
with binding units on (2026-11-10, commit
`627ed80e1ffb9768e45128ae19027ab24cd46311`).

## Cause and options

The type of each new `Sin` level reads `isFinite` of its operand. The getter
(`boxed-expression/boxed-function.ts`) is not cached, and for `Sin` it reads
`op1.isFinite` down the whole chain, with three reads of the cached type of
each level. With binding units on, each type-cache hit also checks and merges
three recorded reads, which costs about nine times a hit without them.

Fix: cache `isFinite` on the node with the keys of the type cache. This
removes the quadratic walk with and without binding units.
