The derivative of a quotient nested six times takes more than a second, and then the engine gives up and leaves it unevaluated.

## Witness

```ts
function nq(depth) { let e = 'x';
  for (let i = 0; i < depth; i++) e = ['Divide', ['Add', 'x', e], ['Add', 1, ['Multiply', 'x', e]]];
  return e; }
ce.box(['D', nq(depth), 'x']).evaluate();
```

Measured on commit `40ba059a8444959fbcfb94fe393d1a540d423088`, on a loaded
machine, a new engine per depth:

| depth | Time | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | about 0.55 s | a quotient |
| 5 | about 1.4 s | `D(…)` unevaluated |
| 6 | about 1.3 to 1.5 s | `D(…)` unevaluated |

A CPU profile of depth 6: 1.30 s of the 1.38 s in `differentiate` is in
`expandProducts` (`boxed-expression/arithmetic-mul-div.ts`), called from the
`mul` of the product rule. Test:
`test/compute-engine/ce-1117-list-derivative-limit-per-element.test.ts`
(`nestedQuotient(6)`). `symbolic/derivative.ts` (near line 1250) already notes
"about one second" per decline at the size limit.

## Cause and options

`differentiate()` (`symbolic/derivative.ts`) checks the size limit
(`MAX_DERIVATIVE_NODES`, 25,000) after it built each result, and the
products it builds are expanded on the way.

- (a) Do not expand products while differentiating; the size check then sees
  the unexpanded result, which is smaller, and the decline (or the result)
  comes sooner. Size S to M; it can change printed forms of derivatives.
- (b) Estimate the size before the expansion and decline then. Size S.
