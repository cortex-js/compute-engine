Creating an expression with eight nested folds takes 44 ms in version 0.182.0, 1.7 times longer than in version 0.156.0.

## Witness

```
ce.box(['Function', RSK_ROW_INSERTION, '_raw'])
```

Fixture: `test/compute-engine/fixtures/rsk-row-insertion.ts`. Published
bundles: 25 ms on 0.156.0, 44 ms on 0.182.0 (fastest of 15, 2026-11-07).

## Cause and options

The passes of the fold refinement are most of the time. The moves of the type
cache key explain about 20%: with no `inference-settled` advance, 54 ms
instead of 68 ms from source. Measurements:
`docs/plans/2026-11-07-ce-1026-nested-fold-boxing-cost.md`.

- (a) Close and keep the cost (recommended). No change for users.
- (b) One advance per fold step: about 9% faster; some inferred types become
  wider.
- (c) Record every read of a scope binding (stopped as CE-535): at most 20%.

If nothing is decided, the issue stays open.

## Decision

User decision 2026-11-07: (a)
