Creating an expression with eight nested folds still takes about 71 ms, 2.4 times longer than in version 0.156.0.

## Witness

```
ce.box(['Function', RSK_ROW_INSERTION, '_raw'])
```

Fixture: `test/compute-engine/fixtures/rsk-row-insertion.ts`. 30 ms on 0.156.0,
92 ms before CE-689, 71 ms after (fastest of 12 warm runs, 2026-11-06).

## Cause and options

Of 1,862 type computations caused by a moved cache key, 1,807 gave the same
type. The key moves about 100 times per boxing, nearly always by the deferred
`inference-settled` event (`engine-configuration-lifecycle.ts`) after a use
narrows a parameter of the new literal of a fold pass.

- A key per binding: the design that the user stopped (CE-535,
  `docs/plans/2026-11-06-ce-535-531-binding-keyed-memos.md` §7).
- One advance per fold step (`asComputation()`): 9% faster, and inferred types
  can change.
- Fewer passes for a rejected fold: a prediction from the admission binding is
  not sound (55 wrong rejections in `fold-nested-refinement-cost.test.ts`).
