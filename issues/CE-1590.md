A Tycho user cannot compute a t critical value, a p-value of a t, chi-square or F test, or a gamma or beta probability.

## Request

The same `PDF`, `CDF` and `Quantile` support as the five existing
distributions, for:

```
StudentTDistribution(nu)            ChiSquareDistribution(k)
FRatioDistribution(d1, d2)          GammaDistribution(shape, scale)
BetaDistribution(a, b)              GeometricDistribution(p)
NegativeBinomialDistribution(r, p)  HypergeometricDistribution(n, K, N)
```

The names follow the existing `…Distribution` heads; CE chooses the final
names and the conventions (the support of the geometric distribution), and
documents them. A density with a closed form evaluates to it for a symbolic
point, as `PDF(NormalDistribution(0, 1), x)` does today, so that a plot row
compiles.

## Witness

On 0.194.3 every one of the eight stays unevaluated, for example
`CDF(StudentTDistribution(10), 2.228138852)` and
`Quantile(StudentTDistribution(10), 0.975)`. Control: `CDF` and `Quantile` of
`NormalDistribution(0, 1)` give 0.975 and 1.959964. Tycho's Desmos importer
writes the t density out as a Gamma formula and has no t CDF or quantile.

## Cause and options

The CDFs need the regularized incomplete beta and gamma functions, and the
quantiles a root search on them. Size M.

## CE answer

Fixed (state `fixed`; Tycho deletes the file when the fix verifies). The
names are those of the request. Conventions, as in Wolfram:
`GammaDistribution(α, θ)` takes shape and scale; `GeometricDistribution(p)`
and `NegativeBinomialDistribution(n, p)` count the failures before the first
(the n-th) success, support {0, 1, …}, with `0 < p ≤ 1` and `n` a positive
integer; `HypergeometricDistribution(n, K, N)` is n draws from N items of
which K are successes. A continuous density is 0 at the ends of its support
(x = 0 for χ², F and gamma; 0 and 1 for beta). A continuous quantile has no
closed form: with exact operands it stays unevaluated, `.N()` (or a float
operand) gives the value. The hypergeometric PDF, CDF and quantile need
literal parameters, and its CDF at a symbolic point stays unevaluated.
