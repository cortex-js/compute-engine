What a variable holds after it is assigned a collection, and what a list that is computed when read gives when it is read twice, is not defined.

## Witness

The probes are in
`docs/plans/2026-10-20-lazy-view-read-capture-probes.md`.

A reproduction, checked at `7fa6178d79e2248af09fdd9b58f8bfa008d9734c` (2026-11-10): the
value that a lazy collection captures depends on whether its free name had a
value when the collection was boxed.

```
const f = ce.box(['Filter', ['Range', 1, 'PositiveInfinity'],
                  ['Function', ['Greater', 'c', 'k'], 'c']]);
ce.assign('k', 3);
ce.box(['Assign', 'big', f]).evaluate();
ce.assign('k', 10);
ce.box(['First', 'big']).evaluate()      ->  11
same, with ce.assign('k', 3) before the boxing of f   ->  4
```

Slice 1 of binding units (`docs/plans/2026-11-09-binding-units-slice-1-spec.md`)
removes the declaration that the first boxing makes, which is one cause of
the difference.

## Cause and options

Define the ordinary capture of a value on assignment, the boundaries of
mutable objects, and the behavior of a view that is read more than once or
whose read has effects. This is question Q11 of
`docs/plans/2026-10-20-bare-tuple-record-any.md`. A design document comes
first.
