A condition that has an "or" at its top cannot be proven from the assumptions, so 87 identities of the Fungrim collection stay unchecked.

## Witness

```
87 complex-domain Fungrim entries whose guards have an Or at the root are not discharged
census: scripts/fungrim/guard-census.json (89.6 % of the complex-domain guards dischargeable)
```

## Cause and options

The assumptions design does not cover disjunction
(`docs/fungrim/FUNGRIM-PLAN-3-ASSUMPTIONS.md` §7, non-goals). Options: case
splitting, or watched-disjunct propagation. About 43 other failures are
symbolic bounds (`|z| < φ−1`), which the assume-side decomposition drops on
purpose. Approved 2026-11-01, to do when there is demand. Probability
`medium`, not `high`: the corpus is a reference set of identities, not the
programs of users.
