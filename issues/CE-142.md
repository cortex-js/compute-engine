`simplify()` of the divergent series 1 − 2 + 4 − 8 + … gives complex infinity, although its partial sums are real.

## Witness

```
simplify(\sum_{k=0}^\infty (-2)^k)   gives ~oo (ComplexInfinity)
evaluate(\sum_{k=0}^\infty (-2)^k)   stays unevaluated
```

The same holds for the form `\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}`. The partial sums are real
and oscillate, so complex infinity does not describe the series; the result
should stay unevaluated, as with `evaluate()`.

## Cause and options

Find the rule of the `Sum` simplification (`symbolic/simplify-sum.ts` or the
geometric-series closed form) that gives `ComplexInfinity` for a ratio `r`
with `|r| > 1` and `r < 0`, and make it decline. Size S.

The other parts of the old entry are separate issues: CE-961 (`\sum x^k` with
no index) and CE-962 (`simplify()` does not telescope).
