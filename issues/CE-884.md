The library may take longer to load than it should, because of the size of its bundle.

## Witness

The entry names "cold-start bundle size" next to six benchmark cases slower
than 0.73.0 (CE-349), with no measurement and no target.

## Cause and options

Measure the cold-start time and the bundle size against an older release
before any work. If there is no measured problem, this is a performance idea
and goes back to `ROADMAP.md`, or the board closes it.
