In Tycho's completion menu, a name found by its humps (`idM` → identityMatrix) shows no bold letters.

## Today

CE 3e8e45745 (on main, not released) adds camelCase-hump and subsequence
matches after the prefix matches. `EpsilCompletionItem` does not say which
characters of `label` matched, so Tycho's menu (CodeMirror `getMatch`)
bolds only a typed prefix, and a hump match shows plain.

## Request (shape agreed)

A field `matchRanges?: readonly [start: number, end: number][]` on
`EpsilCompletionItem`: the characters of `label` that the typed text
matched, as offsets into `label`, end excluded, sorted, not overlapping.
Absent means a prefix match of the typed text (`range[0]` to the caret).
Tycho turns the ranges into CodeMirror's flat `getMatch` list, and matches
its own document names the same way.

## Instrument

`node scripts/repros/2026-11-21-ce1695-completion-match-ranges.mjs` (Tycho)
prints `CE-1695: READY` when, at `let a = idM`, `identityMatrix` has
`matchRanges` that cover offsets 0, 1 and 8, and, at `let a = ident`, it has
no `matchRanges` or ranges that cover offsets 0 to 4. On 0.198.0:
`CE-1695: NOT READY` (no hump matches yet, so `identityMatrix` is not offered
at `idM`).

Tycho half on adoption: `getMatch` in `epsil-editor.ts` reads `matchRanges`,
and `epsilCompletionList` (`epsil-editor-slot.ts`) matches the document names
by humps too.
