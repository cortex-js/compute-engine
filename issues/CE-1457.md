The integral of the density of an exponential distribution stays unevaluated, while the normal and uniform densities integrate.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 9777d22c1: `integrate(pdf(exponentialDistribution(2), x), (x, 0, oo))` stays
`int_(0)^(+oo)(PDF(ExponentialDistribution(2), x) dx)`, and the indefinite form
stays unevaluated too. `pdf(exponentialDistribution(2), x)` evaluates to
`Which(x < 0, 0, true, 2e^(-2x))`, and `integrate(2e^(-2x), (x, 0, oo))` is 1;
`integrate(pdf(uniformDistribution(0, 2), x), (x, 0, 2))` is 1.

## Cause and options

The CE-1379 rule replaces `PDF` by its closed form, but `Integrate` does not
integrate a `Which` closed form (a density with a support). Fix: split a
`Which` integrand over the limits at its conditions (here `x < 0` against
`[0, ∞)`), or give the distribution a closed form on its support for
`Integrate`. Workaround: write the closed form.
