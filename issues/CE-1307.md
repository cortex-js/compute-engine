The integral of a polynomial written as a high power, times `e^x`, does not return: the page freezes.

## Witness

At commit `ce7bbd0bdf42f78414e81b26c06d6ab67d5234db`:

```ts
const ce = new ComputeEngine();
ce.parse('\\int (x^{65536}+1)^{65536} e^{x}\\,dx').evaluate();
// no result after 100 s
```

The polynomial has degree 2³², written in a few characters.

## Cause and options

Not investigated. The rule of CE-1282 for `p(x)·e^{ax+b}` reads the degree
with `polynomialDegree()` and declines above degree 200, so it is not the
cause; another step of the antiderivative search, or a simplification of the
integrand, expands the power or walks it without a bound on its size.

- (a) Find the step and give it a bound on the degree or the size of the
  expansion, with a decline (the integral stays unevaluated).
