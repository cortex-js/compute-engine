Cutting a list of unknown length into chunks, or splitting a list where a condition holds, does not compile.

## Witness

On main b40a45941, javascript: `Chunk(L, k)` and `Partition(L, n)` with `L:
list<real>` (the census case), and `Partition(L, predicate)`, decline
("rows can have different lengths"). 0.193.0 compiled them. With a stated
length and chunks of one length they compile.

## Cause and options

The IR has no shape for a list of lists whose rows differ in length: a list
of lists has one inner length. Supporting it needs a ragged-list shape
across lowering, the verifier and the printers, a change to the core of the
compiler, to be designed and judged on its own.
