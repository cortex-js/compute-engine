The integral `\int \frac{dx}{x}` reports an undeclared symbol `d` when diagnostics are on.

## Witness

With the parse option `diagnostics: true`, `\int \frac{dx}{x}` reports an
undeclared symbol `d`. `\int dx` already parses to `Integrate(1, x)`.

## Cause and options

A `d` is pruned from the report of free symbols only where the integration
variable follows it directly (`dx`). The pruning does not know the `d` of a
differential inside a fraction. Pruning every `d` in the span of the integrand
would hide a real free variable named `d`. The parser is `parseIntegral()` in
`latex-syntax/dictionary/definitions-calculus.ts`. This file holds the former
sub-item CE-291.2, the only one left.
