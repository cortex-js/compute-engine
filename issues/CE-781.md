The derivative of a definite integral with respect to a variable other than the integration variable stays unevaluated.

## Witness

```
\frac{d}{dy}\int_0^1 xy\,dx    evaluate gives D(∫_0^1 x·y dx, y), should be 1/2
```

## Cause and options

`D` has no rule for an `Integrate` whose limits do not depend on the
variable of differentiation. Options: (a) differentiate under the integral
sign (Leibniz rule: `D(∫_a^b f dx, y) = ∫_a^b ∂f/∂y dx`, plus the boundary
terms when `a` or `b` depend on `y`), then evaluate the new integral; (b)
evaluate the integral first when it has a closed form, then differentiate.
(a) also covers integrals with no closed form. Size S.
