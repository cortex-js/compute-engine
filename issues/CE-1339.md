Epsil text written for a product nested in a product reads back as a different expression when a factor is `%`.

## Witness

On main at `fed2ad56317cc2a41f1124414bdc7c97d857d83a`:

```
serializeEpsil(['Multiply', 'a', ['Multiply', ['Mod', 'x', 2], 'b']])   // "a * x % 2 * b"
parseEpsil('a * x % 2 * b')     // Multiply(Mod(Multiply(a, x), 2), b)
serializeEpsil(['Multiply', 'a', ['Mod', 'x', 2]])                      // "a * (x % 2)", right
```

The reporter met it reading back a definition held in raw form
(`2^e * (X / 2^e % 2) * Y`). Probability `medium`: a non-canonical nested
product with a `%` or `/` factor.

## Cause and options

The serializer parenthesizes a `Mod` operand of `Multiply` but prints a
`Multiply` operand of `Multiply` flat, so its first factor joins the
operator on its left. Parenthesize a `Multiply` (and a `Divide`) operand of
`Multiply` whose printed form starts with an operand of an operator of the
same precedence, or always. `src/epsil/serialize-epsil.ts`. Size S.
