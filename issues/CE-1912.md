No user-visible change: CE keeps three Desmos-only drawing operators that Tycho now declares itself.

## Witness

On CE 0.204.1 a bare engine declares `Segment` `(any+) -> expression` and
`GeometricVector` `(start: any, end: any) -> expression` and `Sphere`, with no
`compile` handler, and parses `\operatorname{segment}(…)`,
`\operatorname{vector}(…)` and `\operatorname{sphere}(…)` to them
(library/core.ts about lines 12948–12995; latex-syntax/dictionary/
definitions-other.ts lines 1020–1051, "Desmos's geometric vector"). CE was
given them before it had structural types, as it was given `Polygon`. The
instrument prints `CE-1912: NOT READY on CE 0.204.1`.

## Cause and options

Tycho owns its geometry operators (user decision 2026-11-27, as for
`Polygon` in CE-1904). Tycho declares `Segment`, `GeometricVector` and
`Sphere`, their LaTeX entries and their `compile` handlers (tagged `{ __tag, dim, geom }`
records), in every engine it makes; the declarations shadow CE's, so the
removal does not break Tycho. Request: remove the three operators, their LaTeX
entries, their `OPERATORS.json` entries and their docs, as d9f2c55ab did for
`Polygon`, with a breaking-change entry in the CHANGELOG.

`Triangle` stays in CE: `\triangle ABC` parses to it through
`parseGeometryMark('Triangle')`. Tycho shadows it in its engines; the
instrument checks that a bare engine keeps it.

Verified when a bare engine declares none of the three and no such LaTeX
spelling parses to them: the instrument prints `READY`.
