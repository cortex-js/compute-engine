`EisensteinE` of a weight of about 50 or more is a wrong number, or `ComplexInfinity`, when the imaginary part of τ is below about 1.5.

## Witness

On main at `fed2ad56317cc2a41f1124414bdc7c97d857d83a`:

```
ce.box(['EisensteinE', 50, ['Complex', 0.4, 0.87]]).N()    // 8.880525460370372 - 3.8151220112825595i; 8.8805290714079 - 3.8151192202071i
ce.box(['EisensteinE', 100, ['Complex', 0.4, 0.87]]).N()   // 80080.27 + 104512.03i; 49.3281 - 59.3508i
ce.box(['EisensteinE', 400, ['Complex', 0.4, 0.87]]).N()   // ComplexInfinity; -31507089.946 + 13619148.946i
```

Reference values: mpmath at 90 digits with 600 to 1,600 terms (the reporter's
script is in the GitHub issue). Probability `medium`: a rare function at a
high weight.

## Cause and options

`eisensteinE` (`numerics/numeric-complex.ts`) stops at
`mMax = ceil(-40 / log10|q|) + 1`, where |q|^m < 1e-40, but the terms
m^(k−1)·|q|^m rise until m ≈ (k − 1)/(2π Im τ): at Im τ = 0.87 and k = 100 it
stops at the peak. Above k ≈ 150 the terms overflow a double, and `2k/B_k` is
not finite. Fix: sum until a tail bound (the ratio of successive terms below 1,
tail ≤ 2ζ(3)·u/(1 − ratio)) is below the target; cap the term count and stay
unevaluated past the cap; use BigDecimal or scaled terms past k ≈ 150 (the
reporter measured 1 to 16 ms per call at 60 digits up to k = 400). Size M.
