A numeric integral with a power singularity close to 1/x reports an error three times smaller than its true error.

## Witness

```
\int_0^1 x^{-0.9999}\,dx
  .N() gives 10000.00000106 ± 0.00000035 (2026-11-06); true error 1.06e-6
```

## Cause and options

The estimate of the ε-algorithm (`EXTRAPOLATION_SAFETY` times the difference
of two orders, `numerics/endpoint-quadrature.ts`) is too small by a factor of
three for this exponent. Fix: widen the estimate when the two orders are close
to the divergence boundary, and measure the change on the endpoint test
corpus.
