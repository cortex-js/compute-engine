A list of points made by scaling one point by a list of numbers is computed point by point instead of by compiled code.

## Witness

`l: list<real>`, `a: real`, `b: real`. Compile to javascript:
`Multiply(l, Tuple(a, b))`.

0.194.3: declines, "Multiply of a tuple: its value on a tuple is not its value
on a list". The interpreter gives `[(3, 4), (6, 8)]` for `l = [1, 2]`,
`(a, b) = (3, 4)`, and 0.186.0 compiled the same value. Control:
`Multiply(s, Tuple(a, b))` with `s: real` compiles.

Found by the Tycho codegen audit of 2026-11-19: the same MathJSON, with the same options, compiled to JavaScript on CE 0.186.0 and declines on 0.194.3 and on the CE main build of 2026-11-19 16:57, in the document context and on a bare engine. Tycho's instrument `scripts/repros/2026-11-19-ce1560-js-decline-regressions.mts` prints one READY / NOT READY line per issue (READY on 0.186.0, NOT READY on 0.194.3).

## Cause and options

The compiled `Multiply` refuses a tuple beside a list. Option: map the scalar
product over the list, as the interpreter does (each element times the tuple).
