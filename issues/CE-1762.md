A double sum whose index is named i gives a wrong number when the inner range
is written before the range of i.

## Witness

At `da7c4ba6c22694771cc469838aa8f8313d3ed5ea`,
`npx tsx scripts/probe.ts --json '["Sum",["Multiply","i","j"],["Element","j",["Range",1,"i"]],["Limits","i",1,3]]'`
gives the canonical form `["Element","j",["Range",1,["Complex",0,1]]]` and
evaluates to 6. Expected: 25, the value of the same sum with the clauses in
the other order, and the value of the same sum with `n` and `k` in place of
`i` and `j`. With `["Limits","j",1,"i"]` in place of the `Element` clause,
the sum stays unevaluated, with the bound `["Complex",0,1]`.

## Cause and options

The canonical form reads the clauses from left to right, and the index `i`
is not yet bound when the first clause is read, so the library constant `i`
(the imaginary unit) takes its place. Option: bind the index of every clause
before the bounds and sets of any clause are made canonical, as the body
already sees all the indexes. Size S if the canonical handler of `Sum` and
`Product` has one place that binds the indexes.
