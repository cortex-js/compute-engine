Reading one row of an input that is a list of fixed-length rows does not compile.

## Witness

`P: list<list<real^2>>`; `compile(At(P, 1), { to: 'javascript' })` declines
("an index that can be out of range … of lists"). The rows of such an input
can be read only through a build or `Map`.

## Cause and options

An index that may be out of range gives the interpreter's `Missing`
element, and a compiled out-of-range read of a list of lists has no scalar
NaN to stand for it. The caveats of `docs/COMPILATION-MODEL.md` allow
`undefined` or NaN where the interpreter gives `Missing`: a row of NaN of
the stated length (2 here), or `undefined`, would be within them.
