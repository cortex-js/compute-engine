A loop that runs until a condition stops it (a `Loop` with no range) no longer compiles.

## Witness

1. `Block(Declare(k, 'integer', 0), Loop(Block(Assign(k, k + 1), If(3 < k,
   Break()))), k + x)` with `x: real` = 1. Interpreter and 0.193.0
   compiled: 5.
2. `Block(Declare(k, 'unknown', 0), Loop(Assign(k, k + 1)), k + x)` (no
   Break) with `iterationBudget: 1000000`: 0.193.0 compiled it, and the run
   stopped at the budget, the error that Tycho's iteration-budget test reads.

736f19c declines both: "Loop(…): a Loop with no Element clause".

## Expected

A `Loop` with no `Element` clause compiles to a `while (true)` loop that
`Break` ends, and that counts its passes when `iterationBudget` is set.

`npx tsx scripts/repros/2026-11-18-ce1485-ce1486-ce1487-list-alias-loop-replaceat-probe.mts` in Tycho. `CE-1486: READY` when form 1 compiles with the
interpreter's value and form 2 compiles. READY on 0.193.0, NOT READY on
736f19c.
