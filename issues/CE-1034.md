With `diagnostics: true`, the variable of a set-builder, `x` in `\{x \mid x \in A\}`, is reported as an undeclared symbol, and a corpus test now fails.

## Witness

At commit `790fc80f14a97bfdd6065e27de2d96e0500312b3` (2026-11-06),
`timeout -k 30 600 npx jest -w 2 -- test/compute-engine/parse-diagnostics-corpus.test.ts`
fails "no bound-variable false fire outside the skip-ledger" with 10
entries, for example `B = \{x \mid -x \in A, 2 - x^2 \notin A\}` (name `x`)
and `Q^+ = \{q \mid q \text{ is a positive rational number}\}` (name `q`).
At `23db78e092dd67fa2ec95c360ee73dbf35e1b1ea` the test passes.

## Cause and options

Commit `f21620507e44be1ec76042838abae48b34abf815` (CE-537) removed the
variable of a set-builder from `freeVariables`. The test flags a diagnostic
whose name is in `symbols` but not in `freeVariables`. The set-builder
parser (`latex-syntax/dictionary/definitions-sets.ts`) never calls
`_pruneUndeclared()` for its variable. Fix: prune it in the body and the
condition. The failing test is in the full suite, not in `npm run test:core`.
