A function with a pair parameter, saved with `toMathJson()` and read back with `ce.box()`, has an error in place of its parameter.

## Witness

```
const f = ce.box(['Function', ['Multiply', 'x', 'y'], ['Tuple', 'x', 'y']]);
f.toMathJson()          // ["Function", ["Multiply", "x", "y"], ["Pair", "x", "y"]]
ce.box(f.toMathJson())  // ["Function", ["Block", ...], ["Error", "'expected-a-symbol'", "'(x, y)'"]]
```

The same happens for a pattern of one or three elements (`Single`, `Triple`).
Checked 2026-11-06 at `83a2ea70eb0e2d0d4d8ee2ab932c2eefb4f4547c`.

## Cause and options

The pretty MathJSON serializer (`serializeJsonFunction()` in
`src/compute-engine/boxed-expression/serialize.ts`) writes every `Tuple` of
one, two or three elements as `Single`, `Pair` or `Triple`, also when the
`Tuple` is a destructuring parameter of a `Function`. The `Function`
canonical form (`canonicalFunctionLiteralArguments()` in `function-utils.ts`)
accepts only a raw `Tuple` as a pattern.

- (a) The pretty serializer keeps `Tuple` for a parameter operand of
  `Function`.
- (b) The `Function` canonical form also accepts `Single`, `Pair` and
  `Triple` as a pattern.

Workaround: `f.json`, or `f.toMathJson({ exclude: ['Single', 'Pair', 'Triple'] })`,
keeps `Tuple`.
