`trigExpand`, `trigReduce` and `trigToExp` do not work on a variable that holds a formula, nor on the result of another of them.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 3400041f5:

- `let y = sin(2x); trigExpand(y)` gives `y`; `trigExpand(sin(2x))` gives
  `2 sin(x) cos(x)`.
- `trigReduce(trigExpand(cos(2x)))` gives
  `TrigExpand(1/2 e^(2i x) + 1/2 e^(-2i x))`.
- `trigExpand(trigReduce(sin(x)^2))` gives `TrigReduce(sin(x)^2)`.

## Cause and options

The three operators hold their operand and transform it as written: a symbol
is not replaced by its value, and an inner call of another transform is not
evaluated first. Same class as CE-1375 (`D` of a variable that holds a
formula). Fix: evaluate the operand (or substitute the values of its symbols)
before the transform, and fix the nested case, whose output also mixes the
two heads.
