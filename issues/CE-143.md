A contour integral with float coefficients can put a pole on the wrong side of the contour and give a wrong value.

## Witness

No reproduction. The risk: a float radius, center, vertex or integrand
coefficient, such as `1/(a·z − b)` with float `a` and `b` on a circle close to
`b/a`.

## Cause and options

A float is read as the exact value it holds, and the sum of the half-ulp
rounding bounds of the replaced floats is a margin: a pole closer to the
contour than the margin is `undetermined` (`parseContour()` in
`symbolic/contour.ts`, `exactFloatLiterals()` in
`symbolic/contour-integrate.ts`). The margin bounds the coordinates, not the
poles: the root of `a·z − b` moves by about `|b/a²|` times the error in `a`,
and a multiple root by a fractional power of the error. The design: a
root-perturbation bound for each polynomial factor. `needs-review`: the old
entry had no witness.
