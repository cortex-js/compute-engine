A minimum over ten thousand points costs about 15 µs per point at the default precision, where the whole minimum takes 5 ms at machine precision.

## Witness

```
Min(1, 2 − 2·PointZ(C))     over ten thousand points
```

The colour row of Tycho document `s8ishknvhe`, which Tycho interprets because
the row does not compile (CE-249). At machine precision: 5 ms under
`evaluate()`. At the default precision: about 15 µs per element, against 0.23
µs for the same `BigDecimal` operations.

## Cause and options

At the default precision each element is computed with `BigDecimal` through
the interpreter. Next step (CE-337.1): a `BigDecimal` counterpart of the
kernels in `machine-broadcast.ts` that keeps the interpreter's order of
operations and rounding. Doubles change the digits: `2 − 2·0.37731147124215664`
is `1.24537705751568672` at the default precision and `1.2453770575156868` on
doubles. The derived `array` of a list of ten thousand big floats takes 5.0 ms; the
rest is building the `BigDecimal` of each double.
