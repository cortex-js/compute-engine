A double integral with one differential inside a fraction and the other after the integrand, `\int_3^4\int_1^2 \frac{dx}{x}\,dy`, reads the `dx` of the fraction as the product of a variable `d` and `x`, and gives 7/2 where the value is ln 2.

## Witness

Readings of 2026-11-08, at commit
`d9eb725a6c0d79bc7b067a4831f3cb19d2efec41`, with `ce.parse(latex)` and
`evaluate()`:

```
\int_3^4\int_1^2 \frac{dx}{x}\,dy       raw: Integrate(Integrate(Divide(d·x, x), Tuple(y, 1, 2)), Tuple(Nothing, 3, 4))
                                        evaluate() gives 7/2
\int_3^4\int_1^2 \frac{1}{x}\,dx\,dy    evaluate() gives ln 2 (correct)
\int_3^4\int_1^2 \frac{dx\,dy}{x}       evaluate() gives ln 2 (correct)
\iint_1^2 \frac{dx}{x}\,dy              raw: Integrate(Divide(d·x, x), Tuple(y, 1, 2)); evaluate() gives d
\iint_1^2 \frac{1}{x}\,dx\,dy           raw: Integrate(Divide(1, x), Tuple(y, 1, 2), x) (correct)
```

Each of the two wrong expressions has two integral signs and two
differentials, so it is well formed. In the first one, the inner integral
takes `dy`, the outer integral finds no differential, and the canonical form
takes the free symbol `d` as the variable of the outer integral. Both
expressions are valid, and with the `diagnostics` option of `parse()` the `d`
is reported as an `undeclared-symbol`.

The witness is `crafted`: the usual spellings put both differentials after
the integrand or both in the numerator, and both are read correctly.

## Cause and options

`parseIntegralBody()` in `latex-syntax/dictionary/definitions-calculus.ts`
searches the body for a differential (`parseSubintegrand()`) only when no
differential follows the integrand. When one follows, it returns the body as
parsed and the differentials read by `parseIndexes()`.

- `\iint_1^2 \frac{dx}{x}\,dy`: the integral has two signs and found one
  differential. Fix: when an integral found fewer differentials after the
  integrand than it has signs, search the body with `parseSubintegrand()` and
  put the differentials found there before the ones that follow the integrand
  (the written order). `markMisplacedDifferentials()` then runs only when the
  count is still short.
- `\int_3^4\int_1^2 \frac{dx}{x}\,dy`: the inner integral has one sign and
  takes `dy`, because it does not know that an outer integral has no
  differential. Fix: the inner integral records that its body holds a
  differential it did not take, as `finishIntegral()` records an integral
  with more differentials than signs (`excessDifferentials`); an outer
  integral with no differential of its own then makes the inner integral
  again with the differential of its body, and takes the one that followed
  the integrand.

One constraint: an integral that has one differential for each sign and no
outer integral without a differential must keep its body as parsed. In
`\int_0^1 \frac{ax+by}{cx+dy}\,dx` and `\int_0^1 (c+dx)(a+bx)\,dx`, `d` is a
coefficient
(`test/compute-engine/latex-syntax/ce-1123-differential-in-body-of-complete-integral.test.ts`).
