A running sum over the rows of a matrix stays unevaluated.

## Witness

`Scan([[1, 2], [3, 4]], (a, t) ↦ a + t, 0)` stays unevaluated (checked
2026-11-06). Over points, `Scan([(1, 2), (3, 4)], (a, t) ↦ a + t, (0, 0))`
gives `[(1, 2), (4, 6)]`, so the matrix case should give `[[1, 2], [4, 6]]`.

## Cause and options

Not investigated. The source note said to check first that `Scan` over a
matrix is meant to walk its rows. The `Scan` handler is in
`library/collections.ts`.
