A plot row that takes the minimum of a mapped list (a Voronoi diagram) draws 2.5 to 6 times slower per point unless Tycho writes the list out before the compile.

## Witness

Compiled to `javascript`, time per call of the engine's code against the
same expression with the `Map` written out as an n-ary `Min` (medians of 7
rounds of 200 000 calls, 0.182.0, box lock, load 4.6):

```
Min(Map(k ↦ k², x + [0, 4, 2]))                       73 ns   29 ns
Min(Map(k ↦ (x − k)² + (2k)², [24 numbers]))          202 ns   31 ns
Min(Map(k ↦ Max(Map(j ↦ j·k + x, [1, 3])), [1, 2]))  175 ns   29 ns
```

All four targets compile each shape, with the same javascript values. Reading on 0.182.0:
`CE-1019: NOT READY on 0.182.0 (… 2.5×, 6.4×, 6.1×, limit 1.25×)`.

Requested: the compiled `Min`/`Max` of a `Map` over a list of known length
costs at most 1.25 times the written-out form per call, for example by
writing out the lambda per element, as `glsl` already does.

Tycho half on adoption: remove `spliceMapReductionArg()` and the
`Max`/`Min`/`At` branches of `lowerListReductions()` (Tycho's
`src/plot/ce-compile.ts`).

## CE answer

CE (2026-11-07): priority P3, confirmed by the board on 2026-11-07
(`minor`, `medium`, with a workaround). Disposition: pool; the batches take
P3 items in priority order, size M. The requested form fits the compile rules:
the length of the list is known at compile time, so the `javascript` target
can write the lambda out per element, as `glsl` does, with no run-time test.
Workaround: keep `spliceMapReductionArg()` and the `Max`/`Min`/`At` branches of
`lowerListReductions()` until the instrument prints READY on a release. Not
measured again on 0.183.0.

## Fix

CE (2026-11-08): the unroll pass of the compiler (`unrollMappedExtremum()` in
`src/compute-engine/compilation/fixed-width-unroll.ts`) writes out
`Min(Map(f, L))` and `Max(Map(f, L))` as an n-ary `Min` or `Max` of one body
per element on every target, when the length of `L` is known at compile time
(at most 64 elements). Reading of the three witness shapes against the
written-out form, medians of 7 rounds of 200 000 calls: 29 / 28 ns,
30 / 31 ns, 27 / 27 ns (ratios 1.03, 0.97, 1.00). The fix commit is the
child of the `fixed-in` commit. Tycho can remove `spliceMapReductionArg()`
and the `Max`/`Min` branches of `lowerListReductions()` after it adopts a
release that holds the fix.
