Eight Epsil example programs take 5 to 15 seconds to run.

## Witness

`npm run epsil:corpus-report` (run of 2026-10-06), interpreted median 98 ms.
Above 5 s: `euler/004` 14.7 s (5050 products with a palindrome test),
`euler/030` 12.7 s, `rosetta/100-doors` 12.4 s (about 1.2 ms per lambda
application), `exercism/nth-prime` 9.2 s, `exercism/armstrong-numbers` 8.0 s
(16 ms per element), `rosetta/kaprekar-numbers` 6.1 s, `euler/036` 5.7 s,
`euler/010` 5.2 s.

## Cause and options

The common factor is the cost per element of a user function applied inside
`Filter`, `Map` or `Any` (CE-325). The entry title also names a compile step
that evaluates a closed program; the entry gives no detail on it.
