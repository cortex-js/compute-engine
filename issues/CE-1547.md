On the GPU, the cotangent, logarithm and square root of a complex number with a very large or very small part are infinite or NaN where the value is finite.

## Witness

In WebGL2 (SwiftShader), compiled for `glsl` with `w: complex`:
`Cot(w)` is NaN past `|Im w| ≈ 44` (sinh² overflows) and for `|w|` below
about 1e-19 (underflow); `Ln(w)` and `Sqrt(w)` are ∞ above `|w| ≈ 1.8e19`
(`length()` overflows). The interpreter gives finite values.

## Cause and options

The helpers use forms that overflow in float32. The CE-1534 port gives
`csc`, `coth` and `log` overflow-free forms (an e^-|y| expansion past a
part of 20, a series near 0, a scaled modulus); the same forms apply to
`ccot`, `cln` and `csqrt`.
