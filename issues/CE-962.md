`simplify()` leaves telescoping sums and some infinite products as written, while `evaluate()` gives their closed forms.

## Witness

```
\sum_{k=1}^n (\frac1{k+x}-\frac1{k+x+1})
    simplify: the sum (reordered)   evaluate: 1/(x+1) − 1/(n+x+1)
\sum_{k=2}^n (\sin(k+1)-\sin k)    simplify: the sum
\prod_{k=1}^\infty \frac{k}{k+1}    simplify: the product   evaluate: 0
\prod_{k=1}^\infty (1+1/k^2)        simplify: the product   evaluate: sinh(π)/π
```

## Cause and options

`evaluate()` and `.N()` call `telescopingParts()` and
`divergentInfiniteProduct()` (`library/utils.ts`) and the closed forms of
`symbolicSumClosedForm()`. `simplify-sum.ts` calls none of them. Fix: call the
same closed forms from the `Sum` and `Product` simplification, and keep the
`docs/SIMPLIFY.md` rule for a symbolic upper bound.
