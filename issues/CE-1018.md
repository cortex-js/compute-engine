A plot row that draws one curve per element of a long list inside a sum opens about 4 times slower unless Tycho keeps its own route for it.

## Witness

`\sum_{n=0}^{3} 2^{-n}|\sin(2^n x + [0...999]/1000)|`, `x` free:
`evaluate()` and a read of the 1 000 elements take 951 ms; Tycho's route, which
boxes the members, takes 248 ms (medians of 5, 0.182.0, box lock). Reading:
`CE-1018: NOT READY on 0.182.0 (… 3.8×, limit 1.25×)`.

Requested: the elements stay evaluated forms (owner decision 2026-10-28);
reading them costs at most 1.25 times boxing the members.

## Cause and options

CE profile of 2026-11-04 (Tycho log, row 474): `Abs.toNumericValue()` builds
a node for a coefficient of 1 (−35 %); the type check after each evaluation
(−20 %); type facts of an unfrozen union (−10 %).

Tycho half on adoption: limit `literalCarrierFamily()` (Tycho's
`src/plot-core/list-materialize.ts`) to literal lists of different lengths.
