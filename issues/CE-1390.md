A list of rows of different lengths has shape `()` and rank 0, the values of a scalar.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 6be5b5f52: `shape([[5, 2, 10, 18], [1, 2, 3]])` prints `()` and
`rank` of it prints `0`; `shape([1, [2, 3]])` prints `()`. Its type is
`list<vector<integer^3> | vector<integer^4>>`. `shape([[1, 2], [3, 4]])` is
`(2, 2)`. The description of `Shape` (`library/linear-algebra.ts`) does not
say what a ragged list gives.

## Cause and options

Option (a): the shape of a ragged list is its outer length, `(2)`, and its rank
1, the dimensions it has in common. Option (b): an error that names the ragged
operand. (a) agrees with reading a list by its outer length, as `Length` does.
