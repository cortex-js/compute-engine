Boxing, simplifying and integrating are up to 4.3 times slower than in version 0.118.2 (measured 2026-11-06 on 0.182.0).

## Witness

`∫₁² 1/x dx` takes 194 µs on 0.182.0 against 45 µs on 0.118.2 (4.3 times);
boxing `√6x + √2x` takes 1.46 times as long. The measures, the profiles and the
older readings are in `docs/plans/2026-11-07-ce-351-slowdown-measures.md`.

## Cause and options

The commit "CE-351: the Multiply type handler types a product of scalar
factors before its tests for points, collections and NaN arms" gave box 18 %
and simplify 9 %. What remains: the definite integral spends about 40 % of its
time boxing and applying the function literal of `EvaluateAt` and in the
methods tried before the antiderivative; the `Add` type handler has the same
tests before its scalar case that `Multiply` had (not measured). Keep
literal-tier types (decision 2026-09-06). Plan:
`docs/plans/2026-09-06-type-facts-per-type.md`.
