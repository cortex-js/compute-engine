Boxing, simplifying and integrating are up to 4.3 times slower than in version 0.118.2 (measured 2026-11-06 on 0.182.0).

## Witness

`∫₁² 1/x dx` takes 346 µs at `5b2cf702b` against 53 µs on 0.118.2; `√(3+2√2)` simplifies in 119 against 46 µs (2026-11-21, second machine). Measures: `docs/plans/2026-11-07-ce-351-slowdown-measures.md`.

## Cause

Many correctness changes of 5 to 16 % each. Five sessions removed 10 to 20 % per case. The profiles are flat: no function is above 2 % on its own.
