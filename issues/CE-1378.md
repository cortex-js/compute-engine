The quantile of a discrete distribution is computed only under `N`, though the result is an integer.

## Witness

`quantile(poissonDistribution(9), 0.95)` and
`quantile(binomialDistribution(10, 1/2), 1/2)` stay unevaluated with
`evaluate()`; `N(quantile(poissonDistribution(9), 0.95))` gives 14. The
quantile of `normalDistribution(0, 1)` at 0.95 evaluates without `N`
(1.6448…). Main (a28dd290f) and 0.186.0. Workaround: `N`.

## Cause and options

The quantile of a discrete distribution is the smallest integer `k` with
`CDF(k) ≥ p`: an exact value. Fix: `evaluate` computes it for a discrete
distribution when the parameters and `p` are numeric (exact `p` included), and
returns the integer.
