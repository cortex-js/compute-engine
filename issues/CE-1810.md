## Cause and options

CE-1736 added the IR operation `rows.join` (`operators.ts`, the row with
`'rows.join'`), with a JavaScript spelling (`print-js.ts`,
`'[...$0, ...$1]'`) and no GLSL spelling. The test lists the operations that
only JavaScript spells in its `jsOnly` set, and `rows.join` is not in it. The
other operations of the rows lane (`rows.chunk`, `rows.get`, ...) are in the
set, and GLSL declines them.

Fix: add `'rows.join'` to the `jsOnly` set of the test, after a check that GLSL
declines a program that makes the operation (a `Join` of two lists of rows).
