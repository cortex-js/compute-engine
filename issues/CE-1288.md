A double integral of a function with two singular points takes about 2 s, and a quarter of it is spent checking again and again where the singular points are.

## Witness

```
\int_0^1\int_0^1 \frac{1}{\sqrt{(x-0.5)^2+(y-0.5)^2}} + \frac{1}{\sqrt{(x-0.25)^2+(y-0.25)^2}}\,dy\,dx
```

`.N()` on commit `40ba059a8444959fbcfb94fe393d1a540d423088`, on a loaded
machine: about 2.2 s, `6.688639977084065 ± 3.8e-10`. With one singular point:
about 0.75 s. A CPU profile of the two-point case: about 0.52 s in
`interiorPoleVerdict` (`symbolic/interior-pole.ts`), which calls `subs`,
`getPolynomialCoefficients` and `expand` (about 0.37 to 0.48 s each,
inclusive), of about 2.1 s in `nIntegrateMultiple`. Test:
`test/compute-engine/integrate-symbolic-pole.test.ts`, "two points".

## Cause and options

The review found that the moving-pole scan of `nIntegrateMultiple`
(`library/calculus.ts`) runs
the symbolic `interiorPoleVerdict` twice per grid cell, and again for each
part of a box that is cut at a singular point. The integrand does not change
between those calls.

- Compute the symbolic part once per integrand (the polynomial coefficients
  of each denominator in the outer variable), and evaluate only the numeric
  test per cell. Size S.
