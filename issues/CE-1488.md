The Apollonian gasket showcase still computes its circles with the interpreter, because its loop over a list of three-circle gaps does not compile.

## Witness

In the prelude of Tycho's `apollonian-gasket` entry
(`~/dev/tycho/graph-paper-site/showcase/en/apollonian-gasket.html`, field
`prelude`), with the CE-1481 change applied:

```epsil
for gap in [(OuterCircle, RightCircle, TopCircle), …] { … }
```

declines with "a tuple of tuples": each gap is a tuple of three circles, and
each circle a tuple of four numbers. The IR README ("Tuples") documents that a
tuple of tuples declines. 0.193.0 compiled it. Reading the gaps from three
lists of circles instead of one list of tuples of circles compiles and gives
the interpreter's circles within 1e-9.

## Cause and options

The IR has a list of points (a tuple per element) but no tuple whose elements
are tuples. Options: (a) a list of tuples of tuples written out per field
(each gap read as its three circles, each circle as four numbers) when the
lengths are constant; (b) Tycho writes the gaps as three lists of circles.
Workaround: the interpreter route (fallback).
