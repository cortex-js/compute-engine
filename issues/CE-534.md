A recursive rule that replaces a name also replaces the index of a sum, which gives an error.

## Witness

```
(j + Sum(j, Limits(j, 1, 3))).replace({match: 'j', replace: 10}, {recursive: true})
  ->  10 + Sum(10, Limits(Error(incompatible-type …), 1, 3))
(j + Sum(j, Limits(j, 1, 3))).subs({j: 10})
  ->  Sum(j, Limits(j, 1, 3)) + 10      (probe 2026-11-06)
```

## Cause and options

The recursion in `applyRule` (`rules.ts`) rewrites the index of the `Sum` and
its bound occurrences, as `subs()` did before CE-530. A design must decide how
a rule that matches a literal symbol treats a bound occurrence, while the
simplification rules must still rewrite inside the body of a binder
(`Sum(2k, …)` to `2·Sum(k, …)`). Workaround: `subs()`, as above.
