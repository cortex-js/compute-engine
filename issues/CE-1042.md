A sum or a product of a missing value stays unevaluated, where it gave the missing value before.

## Witness

`ce.box(['Sum', 'Missing']).evaluate()` gives `["Sum","Missing"]`, and
`ce.box(['Product', 'Missing']).evaluate()` gives `["Product","Missing"]`.
`docs/ERROR-MODEL.md` and `test/compute-engine/missing-result-only.test.ts`
("AN AGGREGATE OVER MISSING") expect `Missing`. `Sum(List(Missing))` still
gives `Missing`. The two tests of that file fail at commit
`89cb4d2a729ca851dc200ea41ddc8d28e5034a9e` in the main tree, with no change
to `src/`. Found by session `cebatch-783` while it worked on CE-783.

## Cause and options

Not traced. The probable cause is commit
`52f08ddc1e6222ffdb5df3237cffa0e6755af70e` (CE-961: a sum or product with no
index whose operand has a free variable stays unevaluated): the new test of
`library/utils.ts` or `library/arithmetic.ts` can read the symbol `Missing` as
a free variable. Option: do not count `Missing` (and the other error-model
markers) as a free variable in that test.
