A partial derivative written as a fraction with an operator above, such as `\frac{\partial^2}{\partial_{x,y}} f(x,y)`, is not read as a derivative.

## Witness

`\frac{\partial^2}{\partial_{x,y}} f(x,y)` has two skipped tests in
`operators.test.ts`.

## Cause and options

The parser does not read this form of the partial-derivative fraction. Not
investigated further.
