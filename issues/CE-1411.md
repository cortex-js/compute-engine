Converting decibels to nepers gives the same number: `unitConvert(quantity(10, dB), Np)` is `10 Np`; it must be about 1.151 Np.

## Witness

Epsil CLI, main 3400041f5: `unitConvert(quantity(10, dB), Np)` gives `10 Np` and
`unitConvert(quantity(20, dB), Np)` gives `20 Np`. 1 Np = 20/ln(10) dB ≈ 8.686
dB for a field quantity, so 10 dB is ≈ 1.1513 Np.

## Cause and options

Both are logarithmic units, and the conversion seems to treat them as the same
dimensionless unit with factor 1. Fix: the factor ln(10)/20 between dB and
Np (and check the bel, `B`), or an error if the conversion of logarithmic
units is not supported. Note the power-versus-field convention in the
description.
