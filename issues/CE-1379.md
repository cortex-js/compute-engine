`Integrate` and `D` do not use the closed form of the density or the distribution function of a distribution, so ∫ pdf over ℝ stays unevaluated.

## Witness

`ce.box(['Integrate', ['PDF', ['NormalDistribution', 0, 1], 'x'],
['Limits', 'x', 'NegativeInfinity', 'PositiveInfinity']]).evaluate()` stays an
`Integrate`; it must give 1. The same integral of the closed form
`exp(-x²/2)/√(2π)` gives 1. `D(CDF(NormalDistribution(0, 1), x), x)` gives
`Apply(Derivative(CDF, 0, 1), NormalDistribution(0, 1), x)`; it must give the
density. `PDF(…, x)` and `CDF(…, x)` alone evaluate to their closed forms.
Main (a28dd290f). Workaround: write the closed form.

## Cause and options

Both operators hold their operand and see `PDF` and `CDF` as unknown
functions. Option (a): a rule in each that replaces `PDF(dist, x)` and
`CDF(dist, x)` of a distribution with a closed form by that form before it
integrates or differentiates. Option (b): `D` of `CDF` with respect to its
point is `PDF`, as a derivative rule, and `Integrate` expands. (a) covers both.

## Decision

User decision 2026-11-16: (a)
