When the variable of a derivative has a value, the derivative inside a sum uses that value, so with `x = 5` the sum `\sum_{i=1}^{2}\frac{d}{dx}x^2` gives 20 and not `4x`.

## Witness

```
ce.assign('x', 5)
ce.parse('\\frac{d}{dx}x^2').evaluate()                    ->  2x   (correct)
ce.parse('\\sum_{i=1}^{2}\\frac{d}{dx}x^2').evaluate()     ->  20
expected                                                   ->  4x
```

The same at HEAD `ed335ce8` (before CE-1064) and after it. With
`ce.assign('x_1', 5)`, `\sum_{i=1}^{2}\frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}x_i^2`
gives `2x_2 + 10`.

## Cause and options

The evaluate handler of `D` shields its variables from their values
(`withValueShield` in `library/calculus.ts`), so a lone derivative gives `2x`.
The sum then evaluates the value of each term again, outside the shield, and
`x` takes its value 5. Not traced further. Options: the sum does not evaluate
again a term whose value comes from a derivative, or the result of `D` keeps
its variable bound (`rebindEscapingBinderResult()`) in a form that a later
evaluation does not replace.
