A series at infinity of a function with an absolute value, a floor or a square root of a square loses its leading term or has the wrong sign.

## Witness

On main:

```
Series(1/Abs(x), x, PositiveInfinity, 3)            // BigO(x^(-4)); expected 1/x + O(x^-4)
Series(1/Floor(x), x, PositiveInfinity, 3)          // BigO(x^(-4)); 1/⌊x⌋ ~ 1/x
Series(1/(Sqrt(x^2) + 1), x, NegativeInfinity, 3)   // 1/x - x^-2 + x^-3 + O(x^-4)
```

At −∞, √(x²) = −x, so the last one is `-1/x - 1/x² - 1/x³ + …`.

## Cause and options

Not examined in detail; the ±∞ route of `symbolic/series.ts` probably substitutes `x = 1/t` and expands at `t = 0` without the sign of `t` (−∞) and without handling a non-analytic operation (`Abs`, `Floor`). The simple sound fix is the one PR #573 made for a non-real direction: decline unless the function is built from analytic operations, and carry the sign of the direction into `Sqrt(x^2)`. Size M.
