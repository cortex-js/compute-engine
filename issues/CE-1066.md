The simplified form of `\log(x^3) - 3\log(x)` is not 0, but the simplified form of `\ln(x^3) - 3\ln(x)` is 0.

## Witness

```
ce.parse('\\log(x^3)-3\\log(x)').simplify()   ->  -3log(x) + log(x^3)
ce.parse('\\log(x^3)-\\log(x)').simplify()    ->  -log(x) + log(x^3)
ce.parse('\\ln(x^3)-3\\ln(x)').simplify()     ->  0
```

Alone, `\log(x^3)` simplifies to `3\log(x)`. In a sum, the rewrite of the
term `\log(x^3)` is not kept, so the like terms are not collected. A user
who checks an identity by subtraction gets no answer.

## Cause and options

Not traced. The `Ln` route reaches 0, so the difference is between the
`Ln` and `Log` rules of `src/compute-engine/symbolic/simplify-log.ts`, or in
the cost check of `simplify()` that rejects the rewrite of the term. Option:
find the step where the `Ln` sum collects the terms and give `Log` the same
step.
