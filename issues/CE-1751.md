A maximum over a range of indexes, such as the largest of a_1 to a_n, gives
an error.

## Witness

After the fix of CE-1728, `npx tsx scripts/probe.ts '\max_{1 \le i \le 3} i'`
parses to `["Max", ["Error", "'expected-set-membership'", …]]`. The same
error comes from `\max_{i=1}^{3} i`, `\min_x f(x)` and
`\max_{x \in S, x > 1} x`. Expected: 3 for the first two.

Workaround: write the range as a set, `\max_{i \in \{1,2,3\}} i`, or in
MathJSON, `["Max", ["Map", ["Function", "i", "i"], ["Range", 1, 3]]]`.

## Cause and options

`parseExtremumOverSet()` (`definitions-arithmetic.ts`) reads only one clause
`x \in S`. Read the subscript with `getIndexes()`, as `parseBigOp()` does for
`\sum`: a range clause `i=a` with the superscript `b`, or `a \le i \le b`,
gives `Map(Function(f, i), Range(a, b))`; a condition gives a `Filter` of the
set. The serializer (`extremumOverSet()`) then writes the range form back.
Size M.
