In Tycho's Epsil editor, `xs |> take(` and `xs.take(` offer no argument fields, and `take(` finds the optional parameters by reading `?` in the label.

## Today

After a typed `(`, Tycho offers one field per required parameter of the
active signature that `epsilSignatureHelp` returns (`take(` shows
`xs, count)`, Tab inserts them as a snippet). `EpsilSignatureParameter`
gives `name` and `type`, but not whether a parameter is optional or a rest
parameter: `type` drops the `?` and `*` (`transpose(`: `axis1` has type
`integer`, its label text is `axis1: integer?`), so Tycho reads them from
`label`.

After `|>` and `.`, Tycho offers no fields: the piped value or the receiver
fills a parameter that the signature does not name, and it is not always
the first (`xs |> map(` fills the second, `collection<T>+`).

## Request (shape proposed by CE)

On `EpsilSignatureParameter`:

- `optional?: true` for a parameter that can be left out;
- `rest?: true` for a parameter that takes the remaining arguments;
- `implicit?: 'pipe' | 'receiver'` for the parameter that the left side of
  `|>` fills (the slot rule of the completion snippets, CE-1664.7; no mark
  when the slot is unknown), or that the receiver of a member call fills.

An optional parameter is one with a default (`b: real = 1`; Epsil has no
`b?` syntax). A dotted call of a library function (`xs.take(`) is refused
(`dot-call-not-a-protocol-function`) and keeps no signature help; a
protocol member call (`c.scale(`) has it, with `self` marked
`implicit: 'receiver'` (CE answer, 2026-11-21).

Tycho half on adoption: `argumentsSuggestion` in `epsil-editor.ts` reads
these marks instead of `?` and `*` in the label, and leaves out the
implicit parameter, so it offers fields after `|>` and `.` too.

## Instrument

`node scripts/repros/2026-11-21-ce1698-signature-parameter-kinds.mjs`
(Tycho) reads the active signature (`activeSignature`) and prints
`CE-1698: READY` when `transpose(` marks `axis1` and `axis2` optional, the
`b` of `function f(a: real, b: real = 1)` is optional, `sum(` marks its
second parameter rest, `take(` marks none, `xs |> take(` marks `xs` pipe,
`xs |> map(` marks its second parameter rest and pipe, and the protocol
member call `c.scale(` (a `Shape` protocol, a `Circle` type) marks `self`
receiver. On 0.198.1: `CE-1698: NOT READY` (no marks; `c.scale(` gives
null).

## CE answer

CE (2026-11-21, compute-engine-91): P4 by the table, raised to P3 by the user (see Decision); shape agreed
(proposed by CE). Built in `src/epsil/signature-help.ts`, with the slot rule
of the completion snippets for `implicit: 'pipe'` and the receiver rule of
member completion (CE-1663) for a protocol member call (`c.scale(`); not assigned to a release.
Workaround: the optional and rest marks can be read from `label` as Tycho
does today (`?` and `*` after the parameter's type); after `|>` and `.`,
none: offer no argument fields there.
