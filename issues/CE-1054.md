Comments at internal callers of `subs()` say it is not capture-avoiding, and those callers still decline substitutions that `subs()` now does correctly.

## Witness

```
Sum(j + k, Limits(k, 1, 3)).subs({j: k})  ->  Sum(k + k_1, Limits(k_1, 1, 3)) = 3k + 6
```

The capture-guard comment in `compilation/compile-cse.ts` still says "`subs`
... is not capture-avoiding". Search `src/` for "not capture-avoiding" and
"does not avoid capture".

## Cause and options

Step 4 of `docs/plans/2026-10-30-capture-avoiding-subs.md`: per caller, remove
the check with a test of the case it declined, or correct only the comment
when no `corpus` or `plausible` witness exists. A caller that must refuse the
two cases `subs()` cannot rename uses `subsIfCaptureFree()`
(`boxed-expression/subs-capture-mode.ts`). A guard that does not call `subs()`
(`substituteIndexBindings()`, `library/utils.ts`) stays.
